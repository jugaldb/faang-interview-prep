# Every tool on this site

Every tool and resource the guides link to, in one place, grouped by the job it does. Free first in each table.

> **Tip:** Prices and free tiers change. The figures here are as of Oct 2026. Check the tool's own pricing page before you pay for anything.

Official policy pages, news articles, single blog posts and company career pages stay on the page that cites them. Programs and fellowships are on [Programs](../internships/programs.md). Conferences and fairs are on [Career fairs](../outreach/career-fairs.md). Jugal's posts are on [Every Ascend post by topic](substack.md).

## How to use this page

1. Find the job you are doing right now, for example "verify an email" or "practice an OA".
2. Pick the first free tool in that table.
3. Open the page in the last column for the exact steps.
