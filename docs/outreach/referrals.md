# How big-tech referrals work

For anyone about to ask for a referral, or who just got one. Use it to learn what a referral changes, how Google, Meta, Amazon, and Microsoft process one, what to send your referrer, and the lines not to cross.

## What a referral does and does not do

| A referral does | A referral does not |
|---|---|
| Get a human to read your resume. Jugal: "a referral mostly buys you one thing, a human actually reading your resume" ([7 videos on networking](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-on-networking-your-way-to)). | Skip the interviews. One Blind poster's description: "Once a referral is accepted, the candidate still has to go via the normal interview process" ([Blind, Jan 2026](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-does-referral-work-on-blind-0zi3u4lw)). |
| Raise your odds of an interview. 40% of referred candidates reached an interview, vs 3% of inbound applicants ([Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/referrals)). | Guarantee an interview. In the same data, 60% of referred candidates never reached one. |
| Sometimes change the first step, by some reports. Extern's Microsoft internship guide claims "A current Microsoft employee's referral can bypass the OA entirely" ([Extern](https://www.extern.com/post/microsoft-internship-guide)). Microsoft's own internship FAQ says referred candidates get "equal consideration and prioritization as all other candidates during each stage" ([Microsoft university internships](https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/universityinternship)). Prepare for the OA. | Lower the bar. Of referred candidates who interviewed, 16% reached the offer stage (Ashby). |

Referral bonuses exist at big tech, but money is not what makes people refer. When Google doubled its referral bonus from $2,000 to $4,000, referrals did not rise. What worked was asking employees specific questions about specific people ([LinkedIn Talent Blog on Laszlo Bock, 2015](https://www.linkedin.com/business/talent/blog/talent-acquisition/how-google-dramatically-increased-referrals)).

Referrers protect their reputation. Your job is to make referring you feel safe and take 60 seconds.

## How it works at Google, Meta, Amazon, and Microsoft

Processes change and most details below come from third-party guides and employee forums, not official pages. Always ask your referrer: "Should I apply first, or wait for your referral link?"

| Company | Who starts it | What the referrer needs | What you do | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Google](../companies/google.md) | The employee submits the referral | Your resume, how they know you, and a few role-specific details ([Aced on Google referrals](https://www.aced.io/blog/how-to-get-a-google-referral)) | Still apply on Google Careers: "The referral will augment your application, but not replace it entirely." You get a confirmation, and a recruiter may reach out (same Aced guide). | Google lets you apply to up to three jobs in a rolling 30-day window, and you must wait 90 days to reapply to the same job ([Google Careers Help](https://support.google.com/googlecareers/answer/6095391)). Pick your 1 to 3 job IDs before you ask. Candidate reports say referred candidates often skip the coding OA but still take the Google Hiring Assessment, a workstyle questionnaire (details and sources on [Google](../companies/google.md)). |
| [Meta](../companies/meta.md) | The employee submits the referral | Your resume and the email you will apply with (confirm with them) | Meta emails you a link to its Career Profile, where you apply. Aced says up to 3 positions per referral, the link expires after 30 days, and a recruiter replies within 10 days to 2 weeks ([Aced on Meta referrals](https://www.aced.io/blog/how-to-get-a-meta-referral)). A Blind post from Feb 2025 says the revamped dashboard allows up to 5 jobs ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/meta-revamped-its-referral-systembut-is-it-helping-candidates-ezyqb87d)). | Act on the link within a day. Interviewers may ask about the person who referred you, so know how you know them and what they work on (Aced). |
| [Amazon](../companies/amazon.md) | The employee submits the referral | Your resume and how they know you ([Aced on Amazon referrals](https://www.aced.io/blog/how-to-get-an-amazon-referral)) | Still apply on amazon.jobs. Aced: the referral augments your application rather than replacing it. Amazon's own process has four stages: online application, assessments, phone screening, interview loop ([Amazon: how we hire](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire)). | The internal tool and any candidate email are not described publicly. Ask your referrer how they will submit and whether you should apply first. |
| [Microsoft](../companies/microsoft.md) | The employee submits the referral first | Your resume and the email address you will apply with | You get an automated Microsoft email with a link and complete the application through it, using the same email the referrer used ([Blind, 2022](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-do-microsoft-referrals-work-n5t2kskm)). Simplify (2026): "At Microsoft, your referral has to be in before you submit the application" ([Simplify new grad roadmap](https://simplify.jobs/blog/swe-interview-prep-roadmap-2027)). Microsoft's internship FAQ: "No, referrals are not required" ([Microsoft university internships](https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/universityinternship)). | Do not apply first. Simplify reports the system cannot attach a referral after a general application. Microsoft's careers site moved to [apply.careers.microsoft.com](https://apply.careers.microsoft.com/careers). Its Summer 2027 SWE intern postings accept applications in the first week of each month from August through February ([example posting](https://apply.careers.microsoft.com/careers/job/1970393556922922)), so get the referral in before the window you are aiming for. |
| Apple, Netflix, and others | Varies | Ask your referrer what their form needs | Ask three questions before you apply: Does the form need my resume and a note? Do I apply first or wait for a link? Which email will the invite go to? | Check the company page for that company's process ([companies](../companies/index.md)). |

## Apply first or get referred first

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| Microsoft | Referral first. Apply only through the link Microsoft emails you, with the same email address. |
| Meta | Referral first. Apply through the Career Profile link within days, not weeks. |
| Google or Amazon | Either order, but you must still apply on the careers site. Give the referrer the exact job ID and tell them if you already applied. |
| Unknown process | Ask the referrer. If they cannot answer within 2 to 3 days and the posting is fresh, apply anyway. Reviews are rolling, and "Many SWE internships only stay open for a few days" ([Simplify Summer 2027 list](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships)). |
| You already applied before asking | Tell the referrer the job ID and the date you applied. Ask whether a referral can still be attached. |

## Who should refer you

| Referrer | Strength | Why | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| Former manager, teammate, or ex-colleague at the company | Strongest | They can speak to your work. Warm referrals ranked first in interviewing.io's survey ([part 1](https://interviewing.io/blog/how-to-get-in-the-door-at-top-companies-part-1)). | [R1](templates.md#r1-friend-or-ex-colleague) |
| Friend or classmate at the company | Strong | They know you and can answer "how do you know this person" honestly | [R1](templates.md#r1-friend-or-ex-colleague) |
| Alumnus after a 15-minute call | Good | Shared school plus a real conversation | [R2](templates.md#r2-alumnus-you-have-not-met), [R3](templates.md#r3-jugals-referral-ask-after-a-call) |
| Engineer on the team after a call | Good | They know what the team needs and now know you | [R3](templates.md#r3-jugals-referral-ask-after-a-call) |
| Stranger with no conversation | Weakest | interviewing.io found cold referrals net negative at FAANG: "Given that cold referrals aren't zero effort, our advice is to expend that energy elsewhere." | [R4](templates.md#r4-stranger-with-real-common-ground), only with real common ground |

> **Tip:** Jugal's rule is to ask for a referral "only after you've actually talked" ([7 videos on networking](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-on-networking-your-way-to)). A 15-minute call turns a stranger into a referrer who can say something true about you.

## What your referrer needs from you

Send all of it in one message. Jugal: "Make the referral take them 60 seconds, not 10 minutes."

- [ ] Job ID and link (1 to 3 roles on the same team family)
- [ ] Resume PDF tailored to that job, named Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf ([tailoring](../resume/tailoring.md))
- [ ] A 3-line blurb they can paste into the form (below)
- [ ] How you know each other, in one honest line (Google's form asks this, per Aced)
- [ ] The exact email you will apply with (Microsoft matches on it)
- [ ] Graduation month and year
- [ ] Work authorization line, if relevant ([international students](../jobs/international-students.md))
- [ ] Your LinkedIn URL

The 3-line blurb (reuse your best resume bullet; see [writing bullets](../resume/writing-bullets.md)):

```text
[Full name] is a [B.S./M.S.] [major] student at [School], graduating [Month Year].
[He/She/They] [built/shipped X] that [result: number, users, latency, accuracy], and [second proof: internship, open source, award].
Applying to [role, Job ID] because [specific reason tied to the team's product or tech].
```

Filled example (invented student):

```text
Priya Shah is a B.S. Computer Science student at Purdue, graduating May 2028.
She built a payments app for the campus store that processes 400 orders a week, and interned on the backend team at a fintech startup in Summer 2026.
Applying to SWE Intern, ID 7712345, because she wants to work on Stripe's payment retry and idempotency systems.
```

> **Tip:** Use PDF unless the company says otherwise (Jugal, [The New Grad and Internship prep](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-new-grad-and-internship-prep)). If you share a Google Drive link instead of an attachment, set it to "Anyone with the link: Viewer" and open it in a private window before you send it.

## The referral ask, step by step

1. Pick one specific job ID. Read the job description and confirm you meet the minimum qualifications.
2. If you need sponsorship, confirm the company sponsors before you spend a referral (section below).
3. Choose your referrer using the strength table above. Never ask two people at the same company for the same role at the same time.
4. Warm contact: send [R1](templates.md#r1-friend-or-ex-colleague) with the full packet.
5. Alumnus or engineer you have not met: send a connection note ([C4](templates.md#c4-alumni) or [C1](templates.md#c1-engineer-whose-work-you-read)), then ask for 15 minutes ([A1](templates.md#a1-chat-request-after-they-accept) or [R2](templates.md#r2-alumnus-you-have-not-met)).
6. On the call, ask about the team, not for the referral. Good questions: "What separates the new grads who do well here?" and "What would you look for in my resume for this role?"
7. Same day or next day, send [R3](templates.md#r3-jugals-referral-ask-after-a-call), Jugal's referral ask after a call, with the packet.
8. When they say yes, send [R9](templates.md#r9-when-the-referrer-says-yes) so they never have to ask twice.
9. If they say no or go quiet, thank them once and move on. Do not follow up a referral ask more than once.
10. Log the referrer, date, and job ID in your tracker ([follow-up and tracking](follow-up-and-tracking.md)).

## After you are referred

1. Watch your inbox and spam folder. Act on any referral link within 24 hours. Meta's link expires after 30 days, per Aced.
2. Apply with the same email address the referrer used.
3. Thank the referrer the same day.
4. Update them after each stage: OA, interviews, outcome ([K2](templates.md#k2-update-to-your-referrer)).
5. Prepare one line about how you know them. Meta interviewers may ask (Aced).
6. If 2 to 3 weeks pass with no contact, send the recruiter a short status note ([F6](templates.md#f6-recruiter-follow-up-after-applying)). Do not ask your referrer to chase the recruiter.
7. If you are rejected, tell the referrer, thank them, and do not ask them to push back on the decision. At Google, wait 90 days before reapplying to the same job.
8. If you get the offer, tell them first ([K3](templates.md#k3-final-outcome-note-to-everyone-who-helped)).

## Referrals for interns and new grads

1. Find past interns: in the [LinkedIn Alumni tool](finding-people.md#use-the-linkedin-alumni-tool), search "intern" with the company in "Where they work". They know the intern loop. Ask for a 15-minute chat first, then ask whether they are able to refer you.
2. Ask TAs, club alumni, and lab seniors who now work at your targets. They have seen your work, which makes them warm referrers.
3. Ask within 1 to 3 days of a posting going live. Simplify's README: "Many SWE internships only stay open for a few days" ([Simplify Summer 2027 list](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships)).
4. Watch the lists daily: [Summer 2027 internships](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships) and [New Grad Positions](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions). Jugal: "the posted deadline is almost never the real one" ([494 Summer 2027 Internships Are Already Live](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/494-summer-2027-internships-are-already)).
5. Meet recruiters and engineers at fairs and conferences, then ask for the chat afterward ([career fairs](career-fairs.md)).
6. Full intern application plan: [finding and applying](../internships/finding-and-applying.md).

## International students: check sponsorship first

Jugal spent 3 hours customizing an application, got a referral, and two weeks later heard: "Unfortunately, we do not sponsor visas at this time." His rule: "never apply blindly again. Always check sponsorship first." ([How to Check if a Company Sponsors H-1B Visas](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-check-if-a-company-sponsors)).

1. Search the employer on the [USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub](https://www.uscis.gov/tools/reports-and-studies/h-1b-employer-data-hub). Try legal-entity name variants.
2. Ctrl+F the job post for "sponsorship", "US person", and "export control". Jugal: "Thirty seconds of Ctrl+F saves you three weeks." ([494 internships post](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/494-summer-2027-internships-are-already)).
3. In the Simplify lists, skip rows marked with the passport-control symbol ("Does NOT offer sponsorship") or the US flag ("Requires U.S. Citizenship").
4. Put a one-line work authorization statement in the packet ([I3](templates.md#i3-work-authorization-line-for-international-students)).
5. Full rules for CPT, OPT, STEM OPT, and H-1B: [international students](../jobs/international-students.md).

## Ethics: the lines not to cross

1. Answer "how do you know this person" truthfully. Never ask someone to say they worked with you if they did not.
2. Put only true numbers in your blurb. Your referrer is vouching for it, and interviewers will ask about it.
3. Ask one person per role at a time. Asking five people at the same company for the same job wastes four people's time, and each of them is putting their name on you.
4. Make "no" easy and accept it the first time. Every template here includes an out.
5. Do not ask referrers for interview questions, internal documents, or details about other candidates.
6. Do not ask your referrer to pressure the recruiter or the hiring committee.
7. Do not use a referral to get around a hard requirement such as sponsorship, citizenship for export-controlled roles, or graduation dates.
8. If the referral form asks about family or close relationships, answer honestly.
9. Never make the ask about their referral bonus.
10. Pay it forward. Once you are in, refer people whose work you know, and answer messages from students at your school.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Asking for "any role at Google". Name one job ID.
- Asking a stranger for a referral in the first message.
- Sending only "Can you refer me?" with no resume or blurb, which turns a 60-second task into a 10-minute one.
- Applying first at Microsoft, then asking for a referral.
- Letting a Meta referral link expire.
- Spending a referral on a company that cannot sponsor you, when you need sponsorship.
- Going silent after the referral. Your referrer should hear the outcome from you.

## Checklist

- [ ] One job ID picked, minimum qualifications met
- [ ] Sponsorship checked (if needed)
- [ ] Referral flow checked: apply first or referral first
- [ ] Referrer chosen by strength; only one per role
- [ ] Conversation done before the ask (unless a warm contact)
- [ ] Packet sent in one message: job ID, tailored PDF, 3-line blurb, how we know each other, application email
- [ ] Referral link acted on within 24 hours, same email address used
- [ ] Referrer thanked the same day and updated after each stage
