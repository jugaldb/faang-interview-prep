# Outreach message templates

Copy-paste messages for every step of a big-tech search, from the first LinkedIn note to withdrawing an application. Each one has when to use it, the template, and a filled example with its length.

## Before you copy anything

1. Fill every `[Bracket]`. Delete any bracket you cannot fill truthfully.
2. Change at least one line so it is about them. Jugal's rule: "change at least one line so it's about them, not you" ([7 videos on networking](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-on-networking-your-way-to)).
3. Put one job ID in every message that is about a role. A job ID lets a recruiter find your application.
4. Keep LinkedIn connection notes under 200 characters. That is LinkedIn's limit for a personalized note ([LinkedIn Help: personalize invitations](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153)).
5. Keep emails between 50 and 125 words. Boomerang's study of 40 million emails found that range got the most replies ([Boomerang](https://blog.boomerangapp.com/2016/02/7-tips-for-getting-more-responses-to-your-emails-with-data/)).
6. Send at most 3 touches per person: 1 first message and 2 follow-ups. Hunter's data on 31 million emails found three emails was the useful maximum ([Hunter State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)).
7. Read the message out loud before sending. Cut any sentence you would not say to the person's face.

| Limit or rule | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn personalized note | 200 characters | [LinkedIn Help a563153](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153) |
| Personalized notes on a free account | 3 to 5 a month (LinkedIn's own help pages say 3 and 5) | [a563153](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153), [a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/billing/answer/a550555) |
| Messages to 1st-degree connections | Free | [LinkedIn Help a702683](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a702683) |
| Cold email length | 50 to 125 words | [Boomerang](https://blog.boomerangapp.com/2016/02/7-tips-for-getting-more-responses-to-your-emails-with-data/) |
| Subject line | 3 to 4 words did best | [Boomerang](https://blog.boomerangapp.com/2016/02/7-tips-for-getting-more-responses-to-your-emails-with-data/) |
| Touches per person | 3 max | [Hunter 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email) |
| People per company per wave | 1 to 2 | [Hunter 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email) |

> **Watch out:** Filled examples use invented people (Priya, a Purdue CS junior applying for internships; Rahul, a Purdue M.S. CS student on F-1 graduating May 2027; Alex, a backend engineer with 2 years of experience), invented job IDs, and invented numbers. Put only true numbers in your own messages. You will be asked about every one.

> **Tip:** Jugal's own templates appear verbatim with a link to the post. They are lightly edited for this site only where the style rules require it: long dashes, emojis, and exclamation marks removed, and ranges written as "15 to 20".

## LinkedIn connection notes

Use these only when a note adds a reason to accept. Free accounts get 3 to 5 personalized notes a month, so spend them on hiring managers, alumni, and engineers on your top teams. For everyone else, send the request without a note, then send an [A-series message](#first-message-after-they-accept) after they accept.

> **Tip:** People can reply to your note without accepting the request ([LinkedIn Help a563153](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153)). If a Premium member has Open Profile on, the Message button works without a connection or an InMail credit, so use that instead of a note ([LinkedIn Help a545663](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a545663)).

> **Tip:** Premium removes the note limit. Jugal's [How I Got LinkedIn Premium for Free](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-i-got-linkedin-premium-for-free) has a DM asking LinkedIn employees who took the Plus One Pledge whether they know of any Premium offers. He calls it a long shot: send a few personal messages, follow up once, then move on.

### C1. Engineer whose work you read

When to use: they wrote a post, blog, talk, or PR you actually read, and they work on a team you want.

```text
Hi [Name], your post on [topic] changed how I think about [X]. I'm a [year] CS student at [School] applying to [Company] [role]. Would love to connect.
```

Filled example (167 characters; blank template 151):

```text
Hi Maya, your post on Kafka retries changed how I think about idempotency. I'm a junior CS student at Purdue applying to Stripe SWE internships. Would love to connect.
```

## First message after they accept

After they accept, you are 1st-degree connections and can message for free. No 200-character limit applies here, but keep it under 100 words.

### A1. Chat request after they accept

When to use: the default first message to an engineer, alumnus, or manager.

```text
Thanks for connecting, [Name].

Quick context: I'm a [year] [major] at [School] and I've applied to [role] ([Job ID]) on [team].

Would you have 15 minutes in the next two weeks to tell me what the team looks for in [interns / new grads]? Happy to work around your calendar.

If now is a busy stretch, no worries at all. Thanks for reading.
[Your name]
```

Filled example (70 words):

```text
Thanks for connecting, Arjun.

Quick context: I'm an M.S. CS student at Purdue graduating May 2027 and I've applied to Software Engineer, Early Career (ID 8412233) on Google Cloud.

Would you have 15 minutes in the next two weeks to tell me what the team looks for in new grads? Happy to work around your calendar.

If now is a busy stretch, no worries at all. Thanks for reading.
Rahul
```

## Cold messages asking for a 15-minute chat

Ask for a conversation, not a job. A chat is a small yes. The referral comes after.

### M1. Jugal's 15-minute chat email

When to use: the default cold email to an engineer or alumnus you have not met. Source: [7 videos on networking your way to offers](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-on-networking-your-way-to).

```text
Subject: Quick question about [team] at [Company]
Hi [Name],
I'm [Name], [one line credential]. I read your [specific post, project, or talk] and [one line on what stuck with you].
I'm applying to [role] at [Company] and would love 15 minutes to hear what the team actually looks for. Happy to work around your calendar.
Thanks either way, [Name]
```

Filled example (67 words):

```text
Subject: Quick question about Payments at Stripe
Hi Maya,
I'm Priya, a Purdue CS junior who has shipped two Stripe-API side projects. I read your post on Kafka retries and the idempotency-key section fixed a double-charge bug in my own app.
I'm applying to the Summer 2027 SWE internship at Stripe and would love 15 minutes to hear what the team actually looks for. Happy to work around your calendar.
Thanks either way, Priya
```

## Referral asks

Ask for a referral only after a real conversation, unless the person already knows your work. Cold referrals from strangers were net negative for FAANG in interviewing.io's survey ([interviewing.io part 1](https://interviewing.io/blog/how-to-get-in-the-door-at-top-companies-part-1)). How company referral systems work is on the [referrals page](referrals.md).

Every referral ask includes the packet: job ID and link, tailored resume PDF, the 3-line blurb ([R8](#r8-the-3-line-referral-blurb)), how you know each other, and the email you will apply with. Jugal: "Make the referral take them 60 seconds, not 10 minutes."

### R1. Friend or ex-colleague

When to use: someone who knows you or your work. This is the strongest referral you can get.

It runs past 125 words because it carries the whole packet, which is fine for someone who knows you. Over text or WhatsApp, send the first two lines and the rest as a second message.

```text
Hey [Name], hope [personal line].

I'm applying to [role] at [Company] (Job ID [ID]: [link]). Would you be up for referring me?

To make it quick, here's everything the form usually asks:
- Resume: attached (tailored to this role)
- Email I'm applying with: [email]
- How we know each other: [we worked together at X / classmates in Y]
- 3-line summary you can paste:
  [Name] is a [degree, School, graduating Month Year]. [He/She/They] [best result with a number]. Applying to [role] because [one specific reason tied to the team].

Totally fine to say no if it's awkward. Thanks either way.
[Your name]
```

Filled example (135 words):

```text

## Follow-ups

Cadence: if no reply in 3 business days, contact a second person at the company; at 5 to 7 business days, follow up with the first person once; about 2 weeks after the first message, send the final note and stop.

This mixes Steve Dalton's 3B7 routine ([Duke Career Hub](https://careerhub.students.duke.edu/blog/2023/09/07/following-up-is-the-most-critical-part-of-your-outreach/)) with Jugal's rule to wait 5 to 7 business days, follow up once, and "maybe twice max" ([The One Skill post](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-one-skill-that-can-unlock-every)). Full cadence and tracker: [follow-up and tracking](follow-up-and-tracking.md).

| Day (business days) | Send | Template |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | First message | C, M, R, E, or H series |
| 3 | First message to a second person at the same company | [F4](#f4-second-person-at-the-same-company) |
| 5 to 7 | Follow-up 1 to the first person | [F1](#f1-jugals-bump-at-day-5-to-7) or [F2](#f2-bump-with-one-new-fact) |
| About 10 to 14 | Follow-up 2, final | [F5](#f5-final-follow-up) |
| After that | Stop. Re-approach only with something new, 2 to 3 months later | |

### F1. Jugal's bump at day 5 to 7

When to use: reply in the same thread so they see your first message. Source: [7 videos on networking your way to offers](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-on-networking-your-way-to).

```text
Hi [Name], bumping this in case it got buried. Totally understand if the timing isn't right.

## During the interview process

Reply to recruiters within 24 hours. Keep every message to the facts they need to act. For offer-stage emails (asking for more, competing offers, extensions), use the [negotiation scripts](../negotiation/scripts.md).

### P1. After the online assessment

When to use: the day you finish an OA, if you have the recruiter's email. What OAs look like: [online assessments](../online-assessments/index.md).

```text
Subject: OA completed, [role] (Job ID [ID])

Hi [Name],

I completed the [HackerRank / CodeSignal] assessment for [role] on [date]. I'm excited about [team or product] and wanted to check whether you need anything else from me.

For planning: I'm available for interviews [windows, time zone], and [I have no deadlines / I have an offer deadline on [date]].

Thanks,
[Name]
```

Filled example (54 words):

```text
Subject: OA completed, SDE Intern (Job ID 3012345)

Hi Daniel,

I completed the HackerRank assessment for SDE Intern on October 9. I'm excited about the AWS teams you described at the fair and wanted to check whether you need anything else from me.

For planning: I'm available for interviews any weekday after 1 PM Eastern, and I have no deadlines.

Thanks,
Priya
```

- [Day, date]: [time] to [time]
- [Day, date]: [time] to [time]
- [Day, date]: [time] to [time]

[If relevant: I'll use [language] for coding. Please let me know if any accommodations or materials would help me prepare.]

When to use: within 24 hours, to the recruiter (and to interviewers if you have their emails). Treat it as a courtesy, not a lever: we found no data showing thank-you notes change big-tech outcomes. Keep it to 3 lines and do not re-argue your answers.

```text
Subject: Thank you, [role] interview on [date]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the time today. I enjoyed working through [problem or topic], especially [specific detail]. Our conversation about [team thing] made me more excited about [role].

If anything else would help, I'm happy to send it.

Best,
[Name]
```

If anything else would help, I'm happy to send it.

### P5. Expedite request when you have a deadline

When to use: you hold a written offer with a deadline and want another company to move faster. Never invent a deadline. Another version is [S8 on the negotiation scripts page](../negotiation/scripts.md#s8-ask-a-company-to-speed-up).

Some teams cannot expedite: Microsoft's internship FAQ says "We unfortunately cannot expedite your application review for competing offer deadlines" ([Microsoft university internships](https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/universityinternship)).

```text
Subject: Timeline question, [role] (Job ID [ID])

Hi [Name],

I received an offer from another company with a deadline of [date]. [Company] is my first choice, so I wanted to ask whether there is any way to [complete interviews / reach a decision] before then.

If not, I understand, and I'm glad to keep going on your normal timeline.

Thanks,
[Name]
```

Thanks,
[Name]

## Replies to recruiters who contact you

Answer inbound recruiter messages within 24 hours. If you have Open to Work on and stop answering recruiter InMails, LinkedIn emails you to confirm you are still looking and removes Open to Work if you don't confirm ([LinkedIn Help: Open to Work](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507508)).

If a recruiter asks for your expected salary before an offer, do not give a number in your first reply. Use [S1 and S2 on the negotiation scripts page](../negotiation/scripts.md#s1-expectations-question).

> **Watch out:** Before you share anything, find the same role on the company's official careers site and check that the recruiter's email domain matches the company. Never pay, never deposit a check for them, and never send ID or bank details before a written offer through the company's own system. The FTC: "Honest employers, including the federal government, will never ask you to pay to get a job" ([FTC: job scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams)).

### I1. Interested

When to use: you want the call.

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out. Yes, I'm interested in [role]. Quick context: [one line]. I'm free [2 to 3 windows with time zone], or send a calendar link. Best email: [email]. [Work authorization line, if relevant.]
```

Filled example (49 words):

```text
Hi Daniel, thanks for reaching out. Yes, I'm interested in the Software Engineer, Infrastructure role. Quick context: 2 years in Java and Kafka on payments infrastructure. I'm free Tue 2 to 4 PM or Thu 10 AM to 12 PM Pacific, or send a calendar link. Best email: alex.r@example.com.
```

### I2. Interested, need details first

When to use: you need level, location, or sponsorship answers before you spend time on a call.

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out. Before we schedule, could you share the level, location, and whether the role supports [visa sponsorship / OPT and STEM OPT]? Then I can confirm quickly.
```

Filled example (31 words):

```text
Hi Daniel, thanks for reaching out. Before we schedule, could you share the level, location, and whether the role supports candidates on OPT and STEM OPT? Then I can confirm quickly.
```

### I3. Work authorization line for international students

When to use: add this to I1, E1, or E2 when you are on F-1. Say it once, plainly, early. Details on CPT, OPT, STEM OPT, and H-1B: [international students](../jobs/international-students.md).

```text
Work authorization: I'm on an F-1 visa and eligible for [CPT for internships / 12 months of OPT plus the 24-month STEM OPT extension]. I [will / will not] need H-1B sponsorship later.
```

Filled example (26 words):

```text

```

### I4. Not now, stay in touch

When to use: you just accepted an offer or you are not looking this cycle.

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for thinking of me. I'm not looking right now [or: I just accepted an offer], but I'd like to stay in touch for [type of role] around [timeframe]. Happy to stay connected here.
```

When to use: the role is wrong for you but right for someone you know. Ask your friend before sharing their name.

## Keeping in touch

Jugal's long-game rule from [The One Skill post](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-one-skill-that-can-unlock-every):

- Check in every few months.
- Send helpful resources.
- Celebrate their wins.
- Offer value without always asking for something.

### K1. Thank-you after an informational chat

When to use: the same day as the call. Name the advice and what you will do with it.

```text
Hi [Name], thank you for the time today. Your point about [specific advice] was the most useful thing I've heard this month, and I'm going to [action you'll take]. I'll keep you posted on how [role] goes.
```

Filled example (46 words):

```text
Hi Arjun, thank you for the time today. Your point about testing out loud before coding was the most useful thing I've heard this month, and I'm going to do my next 3 mocks that way. I'll keep you posted on how the Google application goes.
```

### K2. Update to your referrer

When to use: after each stage (OA, interviews, outcome), whatever the result. It takes one minute.

```text
Hi [Name], quick update: thanks to your referral I [got the OA / have interviews on [date] / received an offer / didn't get it this time]. Really appreciate you putting my name in. [If rejected: I'm going to reapply in [cycle] and keep improving [area].]
```

Filled example (23 words):

```text
Hi Kevin, quick update: thanks to your referral I have the final round on November 12. Really appreciate you putting my name in.
```

### K3. Final outcome note to everyone who helped

When to use: when you accept an offer. Send it to each person who took a call or referred you.

```text
Hi [Name], wanted you to hear it from me: I accepted an offer from [Company] for [role], starting [month]. Our conversation about [topic] helped me [specific effect]. Thank you. If I can ever return the favor, [how: review resumes, intro to my team, answer questions for your students], just ask.
```

Filled example (53 words):

```text

Thank you. If I can ever return the favor, like answering questions from Purdue students you mentor, just ask.

### K4. Check-in every 2 to 3 months

When to use: people who helped you, with no ask attached. Share a win, a resource, or congratulate theirs.

```text
Hi [Name], saw [their news / post / launch], congrats. [One line on why it's impressive or useful to you.] On my side, [one short update]. Hope the [team / quarter] is going well.
```

## Subject line bank

Short and specific beats clever. Boomerang found 3 to 4 word subjects did best ([Boomerang](https://blog.boomerangapp.com/2016/02/7-tips-for-getting-more-responses-to-your-emails-with-data/)). MIT's international student office suggests "Question from an [School] student" style subjects for cold emails ([MIT ISO: cold emailing](https://iso.mit.edu/americanisms/cold-emailing/)).

| Situation | Subject line |
|---|---|
| Chat request | `Quick question about [team] at [Company]` |
| Chat request, student | `Question from a [School] student` |
| Alumni | `[School] [major] student, question about [team]` |
| Read their work | `Your [talk / post] on [topic]` |
| Recruiter after applying | `[Role] applicant, Job ID [ID]` |
| Referral ask | `Referral for [role], Job ID [ID]?` |
| After OA | `OA completed, [role] (Job ID [ID])` |
| Thank-you | `Thank you, [role] interview on [date]` |
| Timeline | `Checking in, [role] (Job ID [ID])` |
| Career fair | `Great meeting you at [Fair]` |

Weak vs strong:

| Weak | Strong | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Job inquiry` | `SDE Intern applicant, Job ID 3012345` | Searchable, specific |
| `Hello!` | `Your talk on shard rebalancing` | About them, not you |
| `Seeking an opportunity at your esteemed organization` | `Quick question about Payments at Stripe` | Short, plain |

## Checklist before you hit send

- [ ] Every bracket filled; no "[Company]" left behind
- [ ] At least one line is about them, not you
- [ ] One job ID, one ask
- [ ] LinkedIn note under 200 characters (paste it into a character counter)
- [ ] Email between 50 and 125 words
- [ ] Every number is true and you can explain it in an interview
- [ ] Resume PDF named Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf, tailored to this job ([tailoring](../resume/tailoring.md))
- [ ] No attachment on a first cold email to a hiring manager; links instead
- [ ] Their name spelled exactly as on their profile
- [ ] Logged in your tracker with a follow-up date ([follow-up and tracking](follow-up-and-tracking.md))
