# FAANG Interview Prep

A free, step-by-step playbook for landing big tech interviews and offers: what to apply for, how to get noticed, how to pass every round, and how to negotiate.

For students, new grads, and engineers with 0 to 3 years of experience. Pick your track below and open the first page.

## Start with your track

### Intern

For students who want a summer internship. Summer 2027 applications are in peak season now (as of Oct 2026), so apply while you prepare.

1. [How internships work](internships/index.md): the month-by-month timeline and how return offers work.
2. [Finding and applying](internships/finding-and-applying.md): the lists to watch and the week-one routine.
3. [Resume rules](resume/index.md): one page, the right section order, what to cut.
4. [How OAs work](online-assessments/index.md): the first filter at most big tech companies.
5. [Problem lists](coding/problem-lists.md): pick one list and finish it.
6. [Intern interviews](internships/intern-interviews.md): what the rounds look like by company.

### New grad

For anyone graduating in the next 12 months, or graduated with under 1 year of full-time work.

1. [What jobs to apply for](jobs/index.md): role families, titles to search, and your target level.
2. [Application strategy](jobs/application-strategy.md): funnel math, timing, and the tracking sheet.
3. [Resume tailoring](resume/tailoring.md): one resume per job description, step by step.
4. [Referrals](outreach/referrals.md): how referral systems work and what to send the referrer.
5. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md): the core patterns with linked problems.
6. [Story bank](behavioral/story-bank.md): 8 to 10 stories that answer most behavioral questions.

### Experienced (0 to 3 years)

For working engineers moving to big tech or moving up a level.

1. [Level mapping](jobs/index.md#level-mapping-across-companies): find your level at each company before you apply.
2. [How recruiters search](linkedin/recruiter-search.md): show up in LinkedIn Recruiter searches.
3. [Outreach strategy](outreach/index.md): who to contact for what, in what order.
4. [System design: who needs it](system-design/index.md): at 1 to 3 years, the design round often sets your level.
5. [Story bank](behavioral/story-bank.md): stories from real work, mapped to each company's values.
6. [Negotiation process](negotiation/index.md): from the first recruiter call to the signed offer.

> **Tip:** On a student visa or need sponsorship? Read [International students](jobs/international-students.md) before you apply anywhere.

## How to use this in 3 steps

1. Pick a 4, 8, 12, or 16 week plan on [Start here](start-here.md) and block the hours on your calendar.
2. Open the [interactive roadmap](roadmap.md), choose your track, and tick tasks as you finish them. Progress saves in your browser.
3. Pick 3 to 5 target companies on [Companies](companies/index.md). Use their pages in the last 2 to 3 weeks before each interview.

Every checklist on this site is clickable and remembers what you ticked in this browser.
