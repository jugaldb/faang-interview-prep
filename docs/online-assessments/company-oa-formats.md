# OA format by company

For anyone who just got an OA invite and wants the format before opening it. Sixty companies, as of Oct 2026, from official pages and recent candidate reports. Formats vary by role, region, team and season.

## How to use this page

1. Find your company below. Read the Platform and Time columns first.
2. Open that platform's section on [OA platforms](platforms.md) for scoring rules and free practice.
3. Compare with your invite. **The invite wins.** If they disagree, the format changed.
4. Read the last 3 months of reports for your company: the company's [LeetCode Discuss topic](https://leetcode.com/discuss/topic/amazon/) (swap the company name in the URL, sort by newest) and its company page linked in the table.
5. Ask your recruiter anything the invite leaves out: language limits, allowed docs, whether AI is allowed.

How to read the table:

- **Official** means the company's own careers page says it. Everything else is **reported** by candidates, with the month where it matters.
- **Varies by role** means sources disagree. The table gives the most recent sourced claim and flags the older one.
- Company names link to the company guide, which lists the sources and the most asked questions.

## If your company is not here

1. Check [more companies](../companies/more/index.md) for question lists.
2. Search the company's LeetCode Discuss topic for "OA" and read only the last 3 months.
3. Read the platform section on [OA platforms](platforms.md). Most OAs are one of the three big platforms.
4. Ask the recruiter. Use this message:

```text
Hi [Recruiter name],

Thank you for the assessment link for [Role, Job ID]. Before I start, could you confirm:
1. Which programming languages can I use?
2. Can I use official language documentation during the test?
3. Is any AI assistant allowed, or none at all?
4. Is the test proctored (webcam, screen share, ID)?

Thank you,
[Your name]
```
