## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Application.** Apply on careers.twosigma.com (Avature portal). Campus SWE roles posted for NYC and Houston (Oct 4, 2026). Hired into a general Software Engineering role and 'matched to a specific team 2-3 months before you start' (official posting).
2. **Online assessment.** HackerRank. Reports vary: about 2 LeetCode mediums (Aug 2025 intern, Panama applicant); 1 LeetCode hard + 1 medium (Mar 2025 Houston); a 'standard medium difficulty' HackerRank with a company-specific string-decoding task (Nov 2025 NY). The official guide does not describe the OA, so treat it as standard but variable.
3. **Phone / video screen.** About 40 to 60 min in a shared codepair editor. Mar 2025 NY report: 40 min total, the first 15 min behavioral ('Why Two Sigma?'), then one LeetCode medium with a follow-up, last 5 min Q&A. Official: if the fit is not there after a few interviews, your slot may end early.
4. **Technical interviews (first half of final).** Official: up to 3 interview rounds in total; the first half is 'three 60 minute interviews' in a 'shared codepair environment', languages C, C++, Java, or Python. Topics: data structures, algorithms, object-oriented design patterns, big O, tree/graph traversal, recursion, searching/sorting, hash tables, concurrency, possibly a systems-design exercise. Reports: an OOP design round (Connect-7 game, Mar 2025 Houston), a tree-focused algorithm round, hard DP. Sep 2025 NY new grad with offer: after the OA, a 45-min technical round (1 LeetCode hard) and a 90-min technical round (2 LeetCode hards); 'they don't give hints or suggestions'. May 2025 Houston offer: phone screen, then two back-to-back technical interviews.
5. **Second half of final.** Official: additional meetings of about 60 min on 'specific projects and contributions you've made in previous roles', experience and interests. May 2025 Houston offer: three one-hour behavioral interviews. Sep 2025 NY new grad offer: about 3 hours of behavioral/knowledge sessions.
6. **Committee review.** Hiring committee, then a management committee (May 2025 Houston offer report). Not described on the official page.

### Intern

1. **Application.** Summer 2027 SWE internship live on careers.twosigma.com on Oct 4, 2026 (NYC only).
2. **Online assessment.** HackerRank, usually 2 problems (LeetCode medium to hard). One Jan 2025 applicant waited about 5 weeks for the OA.
3. **Technical interviews (1 to 3).** 60 min live coding in a company codepair IDE. Reported: Word Search II (Jan 2025), encode and decode a Huffman tree (Aug 2025), 'a heinous DP problem' in round 3 (Feb 2025). Many candidates stop after round 1.
4. **First-year program track.** A Feb 2025 first-year internship candidate had one video interview with a non-LeetCode task: debug a binary tree.
5. **Offer and team match.** Matched to a team 2 to 3 months before start (official posting). Whole process about 7 weeks in one 2025 report.

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

Same structure (screen, three 60-min technical rounds, then project and experience interviews), with heavier weight on design/architecture and on past projects. The official guide grades 'design/architecture' and may include a systems-design exercise. A Houston 2025 SWE loop added three one-hour behavioral interviews plus hiring and management committees. Expect concurrency questions for systems roles.

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** HackerRank
- **Format:** Usually 2 coding problems (LeetCode medium to hard). Duration: a May 2024 Taro intern report describes 'a 90-minute technical assessment'; no 2025 report states the OA length. 2025 variants: 2 mediums (Aug 2025); 1 hard + 1 medium (Mar 2025 Houston); a company-specific decoding task rated medium where the candidate ran out of time (Nov 2025). A Feb 2025 intern solved 1 of 2 and still reached three technical rounds.
- **Notes:** The official guide does not describe the OA, so format can change. Official: 'Use of ChatGPT or any other AI tool is strictly prohibited' and can lead to disqualification. Hints: the official guide says interviewers are 'happy to give you hints', but a Sep 2025 new grad with an offer said live interviewers gave no hints or suggestions.

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** Official: up to 3 interview rounds; the technical half is three 60-min codepair interviews (new grad). Reports: 1 screen + 2 to 3 technical interviews (2025), sometimes a 45-min and a 90-min technical round. Interns: 1 to 3 technical interviews.
- **Style:** LeetCode medium to hard, plus company-specific tasks (string decoding, Huffman encode/decode, game OOP). Graphs and trees come up often ('they love those', May 2025). Some first-year rounds are debugging, not LeetCode.
- **Environment:** Shared codepair environment with the interviewer (official). Languages: C, C++, Java, or Python (official).
- **Graded on:** Official: 'coding ability, computer science knowledge, testing, design/architecture, and general problem solving ability', with clean, scalable code; consider memory and performance beyond brute force; accept hints.
- **Reported focus topics:** Graphs (BFS, DFS, union-find, topological sort), Trees and tries (Word Search II style), Dynamic programming at LeetCode-hard level, Hash tables, sorting, searching, recursion, big O (official list), Concurrency basics (official list), Object-oriented design of games and small systems, Encoding and decoding (Huffman, custom string codecs), Testing your own code

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## Behavioral

**Framework:** No named framework. Project and experience interviews in the second half of the final; short behavioral segment in screens. ([official page](https://www.twosigma.com/about-us/))

- Passion for data and building quality software (official posting)

## Tips

- Read the official guide (https://www.twosigma.com/interviewing-for-software-engineering/) and watch its 'Software Engineering Interview Demo' video. Pick one of its four languages: C, C++, Java, or Python.
- Train for LeetCode hards under time. 2025 reports include an OA with 1 hard + 1 medium (Houston) and a new grad loop with a 45-min round (1 hard) and a 90-min round (2 hards).
- Drill graphs and trees first; a 2025 offer holder said 'they love those'. Do Word Search II (https://leetcode.com/problems/word-search-ii/).
- Practice one OOP game design (Connect Four style board, win detection, extensible rules) and one encode/decode problem (Huffman) before the onsite.
- Test out loud. 'Testing' is one of five official grading areas; walk through edge cases before the interviewer asks.
- Prepare 2 to 3 project stories with your own contribution, design choices and trade-offs for the second-half interviews.
- Do not use AI tools. Official: 'Use of ChatGPT or any other AI tool is strictly prohibited' and can lead to disqualification.
- Use the official reading list if you want books: Cracking the Coding Interview, Elements of Programming Interviews in Python, The Algorithm Design Manual (all named on the official guide).
- Expect weeks between stages; keep other processes moving and tell your recruiter about deadlines.

## 4-week plan for Two Sigma

Do this after you finish a core list like [Grind 75 or NeetCode 150](../coding/problem-lists.md).

- [ ] Week 1: Solve problems 1 to 20 from the most frequent list. Time-box each at 30 minutes.
- [ ] Week 2: Solve problems 21 to 40. Re-solve any you failed in week 1 without looking.
- [ ] Week 3: Solve problems 41 to 50 and every reported question above. Do 2 timed mock interviews.
- [ ] Week 4: Write 8 stories for the No named framework. Project and experience interviews in the second half of the final; short behavioral segment in screens. round. Do 2 full mock loops. Review the online assessment and system design notes above.
