## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Apply (one application covers Citadel and Citadel Securities).** Official: interviewing means talking with two firms at once; the process is the same for both and placement depends on skills, experience and business needs. Typically only one role at a time (e.g., engineering or quant). Ascend: applications open as early as June to August and many close by September to October. The official application form (as of Oct 2026) asks for your top two interview languages (C++, Python, Java, JavaScript, TypeScript), any late-stage competing processes, and a 150 to 300 word personal story.
2. **HackerRank online assessment (reported).** Campus SWE assessment for Citadel and Citadel Securities: 2 to 3 coding problems, medium to hard, heavy on DP, combinatorics with mod 1e9+7, counting, and graph/design style tasks (2025 to 2026 posts). A May 2025 candidate for Citadel's Launch internship was given 66 minutes. C++ engineer roles at Citadel Securities get a single optimization task (May 2026 report) on unproctored HackerRank (Sept 2026 report): 'refactor and speed up this slow C++ function', with a 100-microsecond timing check.
3. **First round (45 min, video).** Official: coding, data structures and algorithms and problem-solving, plus questions on technical interests, past internships, school projects and why Citadel. You may be asked to explain your approach.
4. **Second round (3 x 45 min).** Official: usually three 45-minute interviews mixing technical and behavioral, with time for your questions. Next steps within two weeks. Rounds 1 and 2 are a general assessment, not team-specific.
5. **Leadership interview(s).** Official: hiring managers across Citadel and Citadel Securities review your resume and feedback; an interested team brings you in to interview with a senior engineer; multiple interested teams can mean multiple interviews.
6. **Final review and offer.** Official: hiring managers decide team fit; if several teams want you, you choose with your recruiter. About 8 weeks start to finish. Interviews run Monday to Friday business hours only; tell recruiters early about competing offers.

### Intern

1. **Apply.** Software Engineer, Intern (US) posting lists Greenwich, Houston, Miami, New York; requires a bachelor's, master's or PhD in CS/computer engineering or related, 'exceptional programming and design skills' and familiarity with probability and statistics. Summer is standard (June to August per the application form, with occasional Sept to Dec or Jan to Apr options); fall/winter internships may exist (official FAQ). Program: a 1-week offsite then 10 weeks of work (official internships page).
2. **HackerRank OA.** Summer 2026 campus OA links went out in Oct 2025; problems reported for 2025 include counting length-5 palindromic subsequences of a binary string, a friend recommendation system by most common friends, and sum of (i mod j + j mod i) mod 1e9+7.
3. **First round + Superday.** Same official campus engineering process as new grads: 45-minute first round, then a second round of three 45-minute technical interviews (a Sept 2025 candidate called it a Superday of 3 x 45 min technical interviews), then team review.
4. **Early-pipeline programs.** Discover Citadel (invite-only 2-day event for first and second years; Dec 2027 to June 2029 grads; US event in New York early April 2027, apply by March 5, 2027), Datathons (60-minute assessment quiz via Correlation One to get in; win cash and eligibility to interview; Women's Datathon Miami early Feb 2027, apply by Jan 11, 2027), Terminal (online team games-based programming competition with cash prizes; short assessment quiz to apply; no upcoming events listed as of Oct 2026), Conference Travel Grant.

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

Experienced engineers follow similar DSA-heavy rounds, often with practical data-stream problems. 2025 to 2026 posts: a 3-YOE candidate in the Citadel NXT process got Find Median from Data Stream in round 1 (rejected next day after missing the empty-stream case); a phone screen asked Implement Trie plus a longest-shared-prefix extension; a 7-YOE onsite asked to merge K timestamped price-delta feeds into absolute prices with a min-heap; Citadel Securities options C++ engineers got C++-specific coding (in-place word reversal with pointer ranges). A 2025 NXT candidate said the HackerRank invite listed 90 minutes with 14 MCQs and 1 coding problem. Secondary guides add system design for experienced hires (low-latency, market data).

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** HackerRank
- **Format:** Campus SWE: 2 to 3 coding problems, medium to hard (a May 2025 Launch internship invite: 66 minutes, 2 questions). Citadel Securities C++ roles: 1 performance-refactor problem, unproctored, with a 100-microsecond runtime check. NXT: 90 minutes, 14 MCQ + 1 coding (per a 2025 invite).
- **Notes:** Passing all hidden tests matters; a May 2026 candidate passed 4/15 cases on a greedy problem and only solved the DP one fully. Expect modular arithmetic, combinatorics, number theory (coprimality, MEX) and DP. Clearing the OA does not guarantee an interview (2025 reports of no invite after passing).

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** 1 first-round coding interview (45 min) + 3 x 45 min second-round interviews, then team leadership interview(s).
- **Style:** LeetCode medium to hard DSA and problem solving; practical data-structure design (streams, tries, heaps); C++ depth for C++ roles. Interviewers explore the boundaries of your knowledge and give hints.
- **Environment:** Video interviews ('we use video, so be sure you're camera-ready'); weekday business hours only.
- **Graded on:** Officially: problem-solving process, assessing before coding, clarifying questions, sharing thought process, discussing trade-offs, being clear about what you know and do not know, taking hints. Plus correctness and edge cases (empty input mattered in a 2025 rejection).
- **Reported focus topics:** dynamic programming, combinatorics and modular arithmetic, number theory (gcd, coprimality, MEX), graphs and adjacency counting, heaps and data streams, tries, strings, C++ performance and STL (Citadel Securities C++ roles), probability and statistics familiarity (listed in job requirements), behavioral: why Citadel, internship impact

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## Tips

- Apply in June to August and finish the OA within days; Ascend notes many quant/HFT roles close by September to October.
- Practice counting problems with mod 1e9+7 (palindromic subsequences, no-consecutive scheduling) and DP until you can pass all hidden tests, not just samples.
- For C++ roles, practice profiling and rewriting O(n^2) STL-heavy code into O(n) passes; know std::find_if, iterator costs and avoiding std::pow for squares.
- Treat round 1 as a 45-minute LeetCode medium/hard with talk-through; Citadel's guide says to assess before coding, ask clarifying questions and take hints.
- Always handle empty and degenerate inputs; a 2025 candidate was rejected after missing the empty-stream case in Find Median.
- Prepare a crisp story on how your internship work moved a business outcome; it is one of Citadel's three official prep steps.
- Underclassmen: apply to Discover Citadel (deadline March 5, 2027 for the US event) and Datathons, which can lead to interview eligibility.
- Tell your recruiter about competing offers early; Citadel says it tries to accommodate deadlines and only interviews on weekdays.
