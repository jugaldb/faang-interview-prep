## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Application.** Apply on deshaw.com (US) or apply.deshawindia.com (India). Official: 'An actual human will review your application.' You can apply to several roles. India hiring is mostly campus: 'Campus recruitment fuels the majority of our hiring in the Technology and Finance functions' (official). Off-campus India OA for 2025 grads ran Feb 2, 2025.
2. **Online assessment.** India (off-campus 2025 grads, Feb 2, 2025): test (platform not named in the post), 120 min, 5 sections: 3 coding problems in 80 min (20, 30, 30 min; first question must be attempted first; no revisiting), 10 technical MCQs (DSA, OS, DBMS, SQL, networks) in 20 min, 10 aptitude MCQs in 20 min; +2 correct, -0.5 wrong. India on-campus 6-month SDE intern OA (Aug 2025): 3 coding problems (two were Codeforces problems), subject MCQs, 15 aptitude MCQs in about 30 min. US: an Aug 2025 report had an HR call, then a coding interview on HackerRank; an Oct 2025 Software Developer report had an OA of 4 LeetCode medium-hard problems.
3. **Phone / screening interview.** Official: phone interview first. About 60 min: project discussion plus 1 DSA problem plus CS questions (OOP, OS, DBMS). India new grad 2025: top-10 leaderboard from a stream, then OOP, OS, ACID.
4. **Technical rounds (2 to 3, virtual or on campus).** Each about 60 to 90 min: 1 to 2 DSA problems (medium to hard, with follow-ups), CS fundamentals (OS deadlock, DBMS indexing, SQL), language internals (C++ virtual inheritance, memory layout; Java), and LLD with class diagrams (furniture shop, Google Classroom, Stack Overflow). US reports: three back-to-back interviews covering CS fundamentals (DP, sorting), math, and a modified LeetCode problem (Oct 2025); a first round planned as 4 hours and cut to 2, with 2 LeetCode mediums plus OS/DP/databases (Oct 2025); a virtual onsite split over two days with some mathematical/numerical estimation questions (Aug 2025).
5. **Hiring manager round.** Resume walkthrough, DBMS (isolation, B-tree vs B+ tree), OS scheduling, a harder DSA problem, behavioral ('A time you handled a critical problem collaboratively', 'Why DE Shaw?') (India MTS, 2025).
6. **References and offer.** Official sequence ends with 'conversations with your references, and, finally, an offer'. US Aug 2025 report confirms a reference check stage.

### Intern

1. **Application (US).** Rolling. Official: firm 'typically completes intern class selection early in Q1' of the internship year; apply by the end of the previous calendar year. Interns in New York, London, Hong Kong, Shanghai, Singapore.
2. **Interviews (US).** Phone interview, then virtual interviews (official). Technical questions span 'probability, mathematical statistics, algorithms, logical thinking, and/or programming' plus behavioral (official guide).
3. **India campus intern: OA.** HackerRank: 3 coding problems + subject MCQs + aptitude MCQs (tier-1 IIIT, 6-month intern, Aug 2025; about 30 students shortlisted). A Hyderabad intern report (Jul 2025) mentions a 3-question HackerRank OA without describing MCQs.
4. **India campus intern: interviews.** 1 to 2 in-person rounds, about 90 min each, mixing DSA (Insert Delete GetRandom O(1) with duplicates follow-up; 2D array plus priority queue), OOP (abstract classes, polymorphism), OS (deadlock), DBMS (indexing), SQL, and a small design task (database table as a Java class with primary key and auto increment).

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

SMT/SMTS (about 2 to 5 yrs, mostly India reports): HackerRank OA with 2 problems in 90 min (DP-heavy, medium to hard; one Mar 2026 candidate scored 15/15 and 13/15 and cleared the OA and screening). Screening: project discussion, 1 DSA (for example Maximum Product Subarray), Python internals (decorators, generators, GIL, async), and a coded LLD (Mar 2026). A Mar 2026 Bangalore SMTS reject had a whole round on Java internals (String pool, StringBuilder vs StringBuffer, pass-by-value, heap vs stack). A May 2025 SMTS loop: OA (Painting the Walls-like + hard combinatorics), 2 code-pair rounds (Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock II/III, knapsack DP, multithreading pseudo code), a design round (browser history, then trade message handling, replay and delivery semantics), and an LLD round (chat application with 5+ follow-ups plus a runnable multithreading task); that candidate attributed the reject to quoting comp near the top band (single report). Jan 2025 SMTS screening: binary search task scheduling, verbal flight-booking/payment design, two behavioral questions. US Software Developer posting is open to all levels.

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** HackerRank (most reports, India and US).
- **Format:** New grad India (off-campus, Feb 2025): 120 min, 3 coding + 10 technical MCQ + 10 aptitude MCQ, negative marking (-0.5), sections cannot be revisited. MT (Sep 2024 OA, posted May 2025): 3 medium DSA + 10 math/probability MCQs + 10 CS MCQs. SMT/SMTS and senior: 2 problems in 90 min (Jan 2025 senior OA: a hard DP on merging two strings to minimize inversions plus an Asteroid Collision variant). US: 4 medium-hard problems (Oct 2025 report).
- **Notes:** Partial credit matters: candidates advanced with 1 full + 1 partial solution. Expect DP, graphs, binary search, and custom-story problems (packets, courses, servers). Some OA problems match Codeforces problems (1927G, 1861E in Aug 2025).

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** 1 screening + 2 to 3 technical + HM (India new grad and MT); 1 to 2 technical (India intern); OA + 2 code pair + design + LLD (one May 2025 SMTS loop); three back-to-back technical interviews or a two-day virtual onsite (US 2025 reports).
- **Style:** LeetCode medium to hard with optimization follow-ups ('a very simple DSA question' became hard on the follow-up, May 2025). Mix of classic problems (Binary Tree Cameras, Number of Islands variant, Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock II/III) and custom ones (top-10 leaderboard stream, submatrix transpose invariant). You are expected to explain and run test cases.
- **Environment:** Virtual rounds use a shared online editor (CoderPad per Techprep, a secondary source). India campus rounds are often in person ('interviews were scheduled offline', Aug 2025). Languages: Python, Java, or C/C++ (intern posting); India rounds often probe C++ or Java internals.
- **Graded on:** Official guide: 'what techniques, concepts, or algorithms you use to pursue a solution, and how deeply you understand their uses and limitations', and 'show your work'. Official traits: uphold high standards, analyze rigorously, communicate clearly and concisely, thrive on collaboration, demonstrate intellectual curiosity.
- **Reported focus topics:** Dynamic programming (stock problems, knapsack, grid paths, partition), Binary search on the answer, Trees and graphs (BFS/DFS, complete trees, islands), Ordered sets, heaps, and streaming top-K, OS: deadlock conditions, scheduling, threads and mutexes, DBMS: ACID, isolation, indexing, B-tree vs B+ tree, SQL queries, SQL vs NoSQL, OOP and language internals (C++ virtual functions, memory layout; Java; Python GIL), LLD with class diagrams; concurrency in LLD, Math, probability, and aptitude (OA MCQs, US math round)

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## Beyond LeetCode

CS fundamentals rounds (OS deadlock and scheduling, DBMS isolation and indexing, SQL queries, networks), language internals (C++ memory layout, diamond problem, macros; Java method hiding, String pool; Python GIL), math/logic and probability rounds (US), aptitude MCQs in India OAs, LLD with class diagrams, and case studies: official process may include 'a writing sample or code sample' and 'a case study'; an Apr 2025 NY report describes a 48-hour take-home case study (the candidate modeled AI work at an insurance company).

## System design

HLD shows up for SMTS and experienced hires (flight booking components, payment gateway, browser history, trade message replay/delivery). US loops may include system design inside the onsite.

## Tips

- Prepare CS fundamentals as seriously as DSA. Most 2025 to 2026 India reports include OS, DBMS, SQL, OOP, or language-internals questions in the same round as coding.
- In India OAs, attempt the first coding question before moving on, budget 20 to 30 min per problem, and skip MCQs you are unsure about (-0.5 for wrong answers, sections cannot be revisited).
- Partial solutions count. Candidates reached interviews with one full and one partial OA solution, so bank passing test cases early.
- Practice 4 LLD problems with class diagrams on paper: furniture shop, Google Classroom, Stack Overflow, chat app (add multithreading).
- Know the internals of the language on your resume. SMTS candidates were rejected on Java String pool and pass-by-value questions; others got C++ diamond problem and memory layout questions.
- For US roles, prepare a math and probability round. The official guide lists 'probability, mathematical statistics, algorithms, logical thinking, and/or programming'.
- Apply early. US intern classes are mostly set by early Q1; the firm recommends applying by the end of the previous calendar year. Expect slow, batch-based decisions.
- International students: the official FAQ says that if they hire someone who needs work authorization, they will work with that person to apply for it.
