## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Application.** Jobs live on Workday: corporate.visa.com/en/jobs 301-redirects to visa.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/Visa, and early careers roles sit on the Visa_Early_Careers Workday site (only 2 software roles listed there on Oct 4, 2026, one being the Belfast placement year). The early careers page lists Sep 2026 campus events (Texas A&M, UT Austin, Harvard, MIT HackMIT, UC Berkeley, Georgia Tech HackGT, UC San Diego). Official fraud warning on the careers page: Visa Talent Acquisition only contacts you from @visa.com addresses and 'will never ask you for any payment'.
2. **Online assessment.** CodeSignal through 2025 and still reported in Aug 2026 (SE I): 4 questions in 70 min, scored out of 600 (typically 2 easy array/string, 1 implementation-heavy, 1 medium-hard). US SDE1 OA (Nov 2025) was proctored with webcam, microphone, and screen share. From Feb 2026, HackerRank tier names appear (Coding Intermediate for new grad and intern, Advanced, Expert; Expert = 3 questions in 90 min). A Sep 2026 new grad OA had 3 questions (2 basic, 1 intermediate). Check your invite for the platform.
3. **US new grad pre-screen.** Sep 2025 report for New Grad (WA, CA): 4 questions (reverse strings by length parity, recipe pattern matching, reactor queue simulation with a 10-slot cooling chamber, a graph problem).
4. **Technical round 1.** 45 to 60 min. Resume and project deep dive (why this tech, alternatives, trade-offs) plus 1 to 2 DSA problems (arrays, strings, binary search, graphs). One Feb 2026 candidate (location not stated) was told 45 min = 30 min technical + 15 min behavioral.
5. **Technical round 2.** DSA (DP such as Word Break or House Robber, graphs such as Number of Islands, binary search on answer) plus design concepts; Jan to Feb 2026 rounds added GenAI discussion. Java roles get core Java and Spring Boot questions (DI, bean scopes, HashMap internals).
6. **Hiring manager round.** Behavioral plus past-work architecture; sometimes light system design drawn on Excalidraw. Feedback often next day to 2 weeks; offer after documents and references.

### Intern

1. **India campus OA.** CodeSignal, 4 questions, 70 min, can be taken from home; questions at most LeetCode medium using arrays, strings, maps (Oct 2025). Another campus OA for the 2-month SDE intern (Aug 2025): 2 easy, 1 medium, 1 hard (neighbor sum, two sorted arrays timing, center-aligned text justification, subarrays with at least k distinct).
2. **Shortlist.** About 11 of about 500 students shortlisted in one Oct 2025 campus drive; the OA is the main filter.
3. **Interview.** One 57-min online interview: project and tech stack deep dive, HR questions, and an easy DSA problem (count repeated words with a hash map). 9 of 11 selected, result next day.
4. **Off-campus / US intern.** Off-campus Summer 2026 OA on CodeSignal (Dec 2025 to Jan 2026, 4 problems); a candidate with 600/600 had no interview a month later (Nov 2025). US 'Software Dev Intern' candidates reported a HackerRank Coding Intermediate OA in Feb 2026. US intern interview rounds are not well documented.

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

For 1 to 3 yrs (Software Engineer or early SSE), India loops run OA + 2 technical + HM; a May 2025 off-campus SDE1 process (6 to 18 months experience) ran all three interviews in person on one day (city not stated). Expect deeper project grilling, Java/Spring Boot internals (HashMap and ConcurrentHashMap internals came up in an Aug 2026 SE I round), SQL, and a design round (rate limiter, inventory service schema, payment states and retries; distributed scheduler at Staff). Some candidates were re-routed between teams mid-process (performance engineering to development, Jan 2026) and re-interviewed. Senior OA (Apr 2026): 4 easy to medium questions, platform not named; Staff OA on HackerRank: 2 medium to hard in 90 min (Jul 2026).

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** CodeSignal (GCA-style, 2025 and still reported Aug 2026) and HackerRank tiered assessments (Coding Intermediate / Advanced / Expert, reported from Feb 2026)
- **Format:** CodeSignal: 4 questions, 70 min (90 in one May 2025 drive), max 600 points. HackerRank Coding Expert: 3 questions, 90 min. New grad Sep 2026: 3 questions. Staff HackerRank: 2 questions, 90 min
- **Notes:** Score cut-offs are unpublished: 567/600 was rejected (Dec 2025) and 600/600 did not guarantee an interview (Nov to Dec 2025). Early questions are simulation and string tasks; the last one usually needs an efficient algorithm (binary search, sorted set, heap).

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** 2 technical rounds with 1 to 2 problems each, plus coding in the OA
- **Style:** LeetCode easy to medium, occasional hard (Trapping Rain Water, Sliding Window Maximum); DP, graphs, binary search, strings, hash maps; India rounds mix in CS fundamentals and SQL
- **Environment:** Virtual (video call with shared editor) or in-person at India hiring drives; Excalidraw used for architecture in HM rounds
- **Graded on:** Optimal complexity (a 3Sum candidate was rejected for O(n^2 + n log n) when O(n^2) was expected), clean code, explaining choices while coding, edge cases
- **Reported focus topics:** Arrays, strings, hash maps, simulation (OA), Binary search on answer, Dynamic programming (Word Break, House Robber), Graphs (islands, cycles, ordering), Sliding window, Sorted sets and heaps, LRU cache, Java core and Spring Boot, SQL, Rate limiting and log processing at scale

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## System design

Interns and new grads: none formally; project architecture questions instead. 1 to 3 yrs: HLD in a technical or HM round (rate limiter, log-based IP rate limiting at scale, API and DB trade-offs). Staff: distributed scheduler design plus cache-latency and feature-flag scenarios (Mar 2026).

## Behavioral

**Framework:** Visa Leadership Principles: Lead courageously, Obsess about customers, Collaborate as one Visa, Execute with excellence ([official page](https://corporate.visa.com/en/about-visa/leadership-principles.html))

**What they look for:**

- Ownership and accountability ('act like an owner')
- Customer focus
- Collaboration across teams ('Collaborate as one Visa' appeared in an SDE-1 interview agenda)
- Execution speed and learning from setbacks
- Motivation to join Visa and fit with the team

**Questions to prepare:**

- Tell me about a challenging situation and how you handled it.
- How do you handle disagreements or different technical approaches on a team?
- Tell me about something you owned end to end.
- What was your most difficult project, and how did you handle production issues?
- Why Visa, and how do you align with its values?

Build your answers with the [story bank](../behavioral/story-bank.md). Company detail: [behavioral guide](../behavioral/other-companies.md).

## Tips

- Finish the 2 easy OA questions in about 15 to 20 minutes; the 70-min CodeSignal format leaves little time for the implementation-heavy and hard ones.
- Aim for a near-perfect OA score: 567/600 was rejected in Dec 2025, and even 600/600 did not guarantee an interview.
- Expect a proctored OA in the US (webcam, microphone, screen share); set up a clean desk and test your camera first.
- Know every resume project in depth; India technical rounds spend 30 to 40 minutes on architecture and 'why this tool' questions.
- For Java roles, revise Spring Boot (DI, bean scopes, annotations), HashMap internals, and SQL GROUP BY/HAVING; these come up for 0 to 2 YOE.
- Prepare a few GenAI talking points (where you used it, limits); several Jan to Feb 2026 rounds included GenAI discussion.
- Map 1 or 2 stories to each Visa Leadership Principle (Lead courageously, Obsess about customers, Collaborate as one Visa, Execute with excellence); a Sr SWE was rejected after an unprepared HM round (Apr 2026).
- Apply through Visa's Workday sites only and ignore anyone asking for payment; Visa recruiters write only from @visa.com addresses.
