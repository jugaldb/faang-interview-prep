## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Application.** Apply on careersatagoda.com (Greenhouse-backed) or LinkedIn, or get referred. Agoda says it aims to respond within a week; no reply in 2 months means it moved on. Agoda says more than 100 openings exist for 0 to 2 years experience across departments.
2. **Agoda coding assessment (HackerRank).** Official: a HackerRank online test. Reports: 2 to 3 DSA questions (IC1 contract OA July 2025: 3 questions in 75 minutes); experienced OAs add an HTTP/REST question (fetch a paginated JSON API and compute an answer). Official assessment guide: tests last 30 minutes to an hour; data engineering tests use Python and SQL.
3. **Recruiter screen.** Team and role overview, your stack and projects, motivation for Agoda, salary expectations, availability, and relocation to Bangkok if relevant (about 30 minutes).
4. **Coding round.** About 60 minutes, LeetCode-style, on HackerRank (durations from SSE and Staff reports; the official guide gives none). Official guide: assesses problem solving, critical and systematic thinking, code efficiency and CS knowledge (algorithms, data structures, complexity). Agoda Engineering (2023 post) says a working solution matters more than an optimal one and you must prove correctness with tests.
5. **Platform round.** Depth in your specialization (official 2025 guide). The 2023 Agoda Engineering post describes it as designing a feature for the Agoda site, app or backend across frontend, backend and data layers, then reviewing, fixing and improving a given code snippet ('play the role of a lint tool'). 2025 to 2026 SSE and Staff reports match: improve a given architecture plus a code review.
6. **System design round.** Architecture with practical design choices and clear reasoning; sometimes framed as improving a partially complete design.
7. **Culture fit / hiring manager round.** Values alignment and collaborative work style; behavioral questions answered with the CAR method (Context, Action, Results).
8. **Decision.** Official: final decision typically within 2 weeks of the last round, by email. Local hires can join about 2 weeks after accepting; international relocation takes 2 to 4 months.

### Intern

1. **Online assessment (HackerRank).** 3 to 4 DSA questions (arrays, stacks, graphs, palindromic substrings, fixed-bound subarrays). India 2025 summer OA: 3 questions (1 easy, 2 medium). One Gurugram candidate claims about 10,000 applicants, about 500 shortlisted after the OA and resume, 66 invited on site (May 2026).
2. **Round 1: DSA and CS fundamentals.** In person at the Gurugram office for India interns (2 eliminatory rounds), pseudo-code on paper in at least one report; 1 to 2 DSA problems (3Sum, hashing, greedy; Group Anagrams and a stack-based parsing problem in another onsite) plus a DB schema design (e.g. PVR cinema), networking (DNS routing), load balancing and rate limiting concepts, and project questions.
3. **Round 2: platform / experience.** Varies: system design for a chat app with read receipts and a SQL schema plus Docker questions; or internship deep dive with DBMS, OS and software engineering basics and a practical DSA problem; or a 15 minute exercise solving an HTML/CSS task with an AI agent where constraints were deliberately twisted so the AI fails (May 2026). Both rounds are eliminatory.
4. **Result.** Reported from 1 to 2 days up to about a month after on-site rounds. India stipend reported at INR 1 lakh/month.

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

Most lateral write-ups are Senior Software Engineer loops (3.10 to 8+ YOE, Bangkok or Gurugram): HackerRank OA (1 DSA + 1 REST API question), a 60 minute coding round (3Sum, Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters, binary search or jump-game DP variants), a Platform round (improve a bookings-by-date-range API with caching, rate limiting, auth, UTC date handling; or complete a partial design and review a given code block), an architecture/LLD round (e.g. build a shadow-testing framework comparing old and new function outputs), and a hiring manager round (mentorship, mistakes, experiments, trade-offs, conflict, relocation). Recruiters share prep docs and links per round (Aug 2025 report). One SSE process (8+ YOE) took 2 months from LinkedIn application to offer. Agoda also runs 'Fast track 1-Day Program' hiring events for Staff backend roles in Bangkok (2026 postings and a Sep 2026 report). New in 2026: a 10+ YOE candidate was scheduled for a 2 hour 'AI assisted pilot' system design round (Mar 2026). Downleveling at offer stage is reported (Gurgaon SSE offered SE, Oct 2024).

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** HackerRank (official Tech hiring guide; consistent in 2024 to 2026 posts)
- **Format:** Interns: 3 to 4 DSA questions. IC1 contract (July 2025): 3 questions, 75 minutes. Experienced: 1 to 2 DSA plus 1 REST API question using HTTP GET on a paginated mock endpoint (jsonmock.hackerrank.com).
- **Notes:** HackerRank function-stub format. Make all hidden test cases pass; one candidate notes correct logic can still fail cases on HackerRank. Agoda's official assessment guide recommends a practice round to build speed and a stable internet connection.

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** Intern: 1 to 2 rounds with DSA. Full time: 1 coding round plus coding inside the Platform round (code review and fixes).
- **Style:** LeetCode easy to medium in live rounds (3Sum, Group Anagrams, Longest Substring), harder in OAs (Count Subarrays With Fixed Bounds, Maximum OR). Agoda Engineering: 'We don't need highly challenging problems'; the focus is approach, testing and readable code.
- **Environment:** HackerRank for remote rounds (you can run code); pen and paper pseudo-code for India intern on-site rounds. Use the language you are most comfortable with. No AI during interviews unless explicitly allowed (official AI policy).
- **Graded on:** Testing skills, critical and systematic thinking, code efficiency and readability, CS knowledge; a working, tested solution beats a non-functional optimal one; proactively discuss complexity (Agoda Engineering blog).
- **Reported focus topics:** Arrays and hashing (3Sum, anagrams, subarray counting), Sliding window and two pointers, Stacks and parsing, Graphs and trees (tree-based OA questions), Greedy and heaps (coupon halving, task ordering), REST and HTTP basics, pagination, JSON handling (OA API question), System design fundamentals: caching, rate limiting, load balancing, message queues, DB replication and sharding, Code review, debugging and refactoring, DB schema design, Behavioral stories in CAR format mapped to Agoda's 5 values

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## Beyond LeetCode

Platform round with code review: you are given a code snippet to understand, debug, improve and lint; or a partially complete design to critique. OA REST API question (paginated HTTP GET, aggregate and return the best result). LLD prompts such as a shadow-testing framework. 2026 intern round where the candidate had to solve an HTML/CSS task with an AI agent and detect why the agent failed. 2026 senior loops: a 2 hour 'AI assisted pilot' system design round (10+ YOE, Mar 2026). Official AI policy: AI fine for preparation and polishing your resume; not allowed during interviews, live system design, technical Q&A, behavioral or take-homes unless stated; no invisible AI tools.

## System design

Appears even for interns: chat app with one-to-one, group and read receipts plus SQL schema; DB schema for a cinema; load balancing and rate limiting concepts. Full-time early career: a Platform round (design an Agoda feature across layers, plus code review) and a System Design round. Official tips for the Platform round: discuss requirements first, lead the conversation, go top to bottom, write it like technical documentation, draw a sequence diagram, discuss trade-offs, metrics and testing.

## Behavioral

**Framework:** Agoda values: Do things the right way; Move fast and be efficient; Be a scientist, experiment and measure; Take ownership and make a difference; Be direct and respectful. Behavioral answers follow the CAR method (Context, Action, Results). ([official page](https://careersatagoda.com/blog/agoda-values/))

**What they look for:**

- Data-driven decisions and experimentation (Agoda runs up to 1,000 experiments at once per its Tech guide)
- Ownership and impact-driven execution
- Clear communication of your thought process; interviews are a discussion, not a Q&A
- Willingness to refine your first answer; optimal over perfect
- Growth mindset, curiosity, eagerness to learn quickly, mentoring others
- Motivation for Agoda and for relocating to Bangkok

**Questions to prepare:**

- Tell me about a situation where your team chose a different approach than the one you suggested.
- Do you prefer building a new project from scratch or working on an existing stable system?
- How would you respond if you were not given the type of work you want?
- Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned.
- Describe an experiment you ran and how you measured it.
- Why Agoda, and why move to Bangkok?

Build your answers with the [story bank](../behavioral/story-bank.md). Company detail: [behavioral guide](../behavioral/other-companies.md).

## Tips

- Read Agoda's official Tech interview guide and the Agoda Engineering post 'Preparing for Interview at Agoda' before anything else; they describe each round and what is scored.
- Practice HackerRank specifically, including the REST API problem type (paginated HTTP GET); laterals report 1 DSA + 1 API question.
- In the coding round, get a working, tested solution first, test both true and false cases, and state complexity before you are asked.
- Prepare for the Platform round by reviewing real code: practice finding bugs and naming improvements in a 50 to 100 line snippet, and designing a feature across frontend, backend and data layers with a sequence diagram.
- Write 5 CAR stories, one per value: Do things the right way; Move fast and be efficient; Be a scientist; Take ownership; Be direct and respectful.
- Follow the AI policy: use AI to prepare, never during interviews or take-homes unless told; invisible AI tools are banned.
- If you are outside Thailand, check the job post for 'relocation provided'; English is the business language and onboarding with visa takes 2 to 4 months.
- India students: watch the Gurugram 6-month SDE intern cycles (shortlisting and OA in November and April in 2025 to 2026, on-site in late May 2026) and expect pen-and-paper pseudo-code plus DB schema and networking basics on site.
