## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Recruiter screen.** About 30 min: background, why Meta, logistics, timeline (Hello Interview E3 guide). Many new grads are contacted after applying with a referral.
2. **Online assessment (CodeSignal).** CodeSignal, proctored with camera, mic and screen sharing. Two formats reported: (1) since mid-2025, one practical problem in 4 progressive stages in 90 min (e.g. in-memory database, cloud file storage service), unit tests visible, reference docs allowed but no AI or solution searching (Hello Interview; Jun 2025 candidate post on the 'new 4-level' OA); (2) 4 separate LeetCode-style questions (a US E3 new grad offer post, Mar 2025: 1 easy, 2 medium, 1 hard; Hello Interview's alternative format: 4 easy/medium questions in 70 min).
3. **Technical phone screen.** 45 min with an engineer in a shared online editor where code is not compiled; typically 2 medium problems, often Meta-tagged LeetCode or close variants; sometimes 1 or 2 short project questions first (Mar 2026 report). Two US E3 offer posts (Jan and Mar 2025) describe going straight from the OA to the 3-round loop with no technical phone screen.
4. **Full loop (usually virtual).** Usually 3 interviews: 2 coding (45 min, about 2 problems each) and 1 behavioral (45 min). Since Oct 2025 one coding round can be the AI-enabled coding round (about 60 min in CoderPad with file explorer, editor and AI chat; multi-file codebase: fix bugs, build a feature, optimize). Hello Interview's AI-enabled post scopes it to E5 to E7 and M2 SWE loops, but its E3 guide says it may replace one of the two E3 coding rounds, and a Bangalore University Grad candidate (Nov 2025) was told the loop was 1 coding + 1 AI-enabled coding + 1 behavioral.
5. **Team matching and offer.** After passing, conversations with potential hiring managers, then offer and negotiation; whole process usually 4 to 12 weeks (Hello Interview E3). Example US E3 (2024 to 2025 cycle): applied with referral Sep 30, 2024, OA Oct 10, all 3 rounds Dec 10, verbal offer Jan 3, 2025, written Jan 13. A Bangalore candidate (Dec 2025) was rejected after the full loop because 'they are out of positions'.

### Intern

1. **Apply.** Meta posts SWE intern roles in the fall; as of Oct 1, 2026 the main Summer 2027 SWE intern requisition had not been posted (Extern; first 2027 req was a Data Engineer intern on Sep 29). Referrals matter; the intern loop is short and standardized (Aced).
2. **Online assessment.** CodeSignal, about 70 min, usually 4 questions of increasing difficulty; some candidates also get an optional hour-long scenario-based behavioral survey (Aced intern guide).
3. **Technical interviews.** Official Meta post (updated Oct 2022): stage one phone screen and stage two interview (video), 'identical expectations', about 5 min intro, 30 to 35 min coding with 'one to two coding questions', then Q&A. 2025 reports: two back-to-back interviews, two Meta-tagged LeetCode problems each (e.g. Kth Missing Positive Number, Interval List Intersections, Custom Sort String, Right Side View). Aced says many recent interns had a single 40-min tech screen after the OA.
4. **No system design, light behavioral.** Interns are not asked system design. Hiring managers are rarely in the intern loop (Aced). Return offers depend on mentor and manager feedback.

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

For 1 to 3 years (E4): OA sometimes, phone screen with 2 coding problems, then a full loop; Meta's full loop guide (PDF titled 'SWE _Full Loop_IC4-6') says '4 to 6 of 45 minute interviews' covering coding, design and behavioral. Hello Interview's E4 guide lists 4 onsite rounds: 1 traditional coding, 1 AI-enabled coding (60 min), 1 System Design or Product Architecture, 1 behavioral; 4 to 8 weeks total. A 2026 Bay Area infra loop (5+ YOE candidate) showed 2 design rounds and the recruiter evaluating across E4/E5. A Bangalore 2025 software engineer loop (2.5 YOE candidate, level not stated) added an 'elimination stage': a live DSA interview, a hard 4-question OA, and a Likert-scale behavioral assessment, before a 4-round loop (behavioral, product architecture, AI-enabled coding, pure DSA).

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** CodeSignal (proctored: webcam, microphone, screen share)
- **Format:** New grad: 90 min, either a single 4-stage progressive practical problem (in-memory DB, file storage service) or 4 independent LeetCode-style questions. Intern: about 70 min, 4 questions of escalating difficulty (Aced). Bangalore E4 2025: hard 4-question OA plus a Likert behavioral assessment.
- **Notes:** Correctness per stage matters more than speed; most candidates do not finish all 4 stages (Hello Interview). Reference docs allowed, AI and solution search are not. OA results are weighed with the phone screen.

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** New grad: phone screen (sometimes skipped) + 2 coding rounds in the loop, one of which may be AI-enabled. Intern: 1 to 2 coding interviews.
- **Style:** LeetCode style, mostly Meta-tagged mediums and their twists (merge two interval lists, left and right side views, calculator with only + and *); about 2 problems per 40 min so speed matters. Meta's full loop guide (IC4 to IC6 version) says 'We do not ask dynamic programming questions'; E3 reports show occasional DP where a recursive solution was accepted.
- **Environment:** Shared online editor; 'the interviewer will not compile your code' and Meta's guide tells you to practice 'with just a plain text editor with no compiler, linter, syntax highlighter, autocomplete'. AI-enabled round: CoderPad with a runnable multi-file project and an AI chat (Hello Interview lists GPT-4o mini, GPT-5, Claude Haiku/Sonnet/Opus, Gemini 2.5 Pro and Llama 4 Maverick). Outside the AI round, no outside assistance including AI tools like ChatGPT, and filters (including virtual or blurred backgrounds) must be off (official guide).
- **Graded on:** Four focus areas from Meta's guide: Communication, Problem solving (reasoning, multiple approaches, complexity), Coding (organized, correct structure), Verification (test cases, finding your own bugs). The AI-enabled round is graded on Problem Solving, Code Quality, Verification and Communication (Hello Interview).
- **Reported focus topics:** Arrays and strings with two pointers and hashing, Binary trees: BFS level order, side views, zigzag, diameter, LCA, Intervals (merge, intersect two lists), Stacks and parsing (parentheses, calculators, path simplification), Graphs and grids: BFS/DFS, islands, shortest exit, Heaps and top K, Binary search (Kth missing positive), Design-a-class basics (LRU cache), Recursion and backtracking (combinations), Reading and extending an unfamiliar multi-file codebase with an AI assistant

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## What they ask (data)

Based on **404** distinct problems tagged to Meta in the last 6 months (78 in the last 30 days, 226 in the last 3 months, 1320 all-time) across two open datasets of LeetCode company tags. Tags are user-reported, so treat frequency as a signal, not a promise.

**Difficulty mix (last 6 months):** Medium 57%, Easy 32%, Hard 11%

**Most tagged topics (share of problems):** Array 53%, String 24%, Hash Table 20%, Two Pointers 14%, Math 14%, Dynamic Programming 14%, Depth-First Search 13%, Sorting 12%, Breadth-First Search 12%, Binary Search 11%

## Beyond LeetCode

AI-enabled coding round (about 60 min): partially built multi-file codebase, often a game or puzzle (candidates and creators describe 'Game of Fifteen' and 'The Puzzle' practice sets); phases are bug fixing, core implementation and optimization; you choose the model; graded on judgment and verification, not prompting. CodeSignal progressive 4-stage OA (build a working system step by step). Product Architecture design round for E4+. Bangalore 2025 Likert-scale behavioral assessment.

## System design

None for E3 new grads and interns. E4 (about 1 to 3 years) adds one design interview: System Design (distributed systems, scale) or Product Architecture (APIs, data models, product features) depending on the role (Meta full loop guide via swe-prep-onsite).

Start with [who needs system design](../system-design/index.md), then the [framework](../system-design/framework.md).

## Behavioral

**Framework:** Meta values plus five behavioral focus areas: Resolving conflict, Growing continuously, Embracing ambiguity, Driving results, Communicating effectively (Meta full loop guide); STAR recommended ([official page](https://www.metacareers.com/facebook-life/))

**What they look for:**

- Values: Move fast; Build awesome things; Be direct and respect your colleagues; Focus on long-term impact; Live in the future; Meta, Metamates, me
- Addressing conflict directly with empathy
- Learning from failure and feedback
- Productive decisions with missing information
- Self-directed delivery and measurable impact
- Clear communication with cross-functional partners
- Honest reflection under rapid follow-up questions
- How you use AI responsibly to ship faster (2026)

**Questions to prepare:**

- Tell me about the project you are most proud of.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a design choice. How did it affect the outcome?
- Tell me about a time you had limited information and still had to proceed.
- What do you feel you lack, and how are you improving it?
- Tell me about a conflict with a teammate.
- Tell me about a time you had to follow someone else's opinion.
- Tell me about a time you used AI to deliver something faster than you otherwise could have.
- How do you use AI safely in your day-to-day work?

Build your answers with the [story bank](../behavioral/story-bank.md). Company detail: [behavioral guide](../behavioral/meta.md).

## Tips

- Drill Meta-tagged problems by recent frequency and their known variants; Hello Interview's most common E3 list starts with Basic Calculator II, Valid Word Abbreviation, Valid Palindrome II, Buildings With an Ocean View and Binary Tree Right Side View.
- Budget about 15 to 20 minutes per problem: state the approach, get a nod, code, then walk through test cases yourself (no compiler).
- Practice coding in a plain text editor with autocomplete off; Meta's guide asks you to practice exactly that.
- Prepare for the AI-enabled round: break the task down yourself, give the AI small focused tasks, read and test every chunk, and catch its mistakes; blindly pasting output scores worse than not using AI.
- Ask your recruiter which loop format you have (AI-enabled vs two classic coding rounds); Jugal's Jul 2026 post notes recruiters tell candidates in advance.
- Prepare 4 to 5 STAR stories mapped to the five focus areas plus one honest story about using AI at work.
- For the CodeSignal OA, practice building a small system in stages (key-value store with TTL and versioning) and prioritize passing each stage's tests.
- Jugal's Part 1 post (Jul 2025) summarizes Meta prep as graphs and trees, hash tables, two pointers, tries, and production-grade code with edge cases.
