## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Application.** Apply on jobs.paloaltonetworks.com. In India, new campus graduates start in the year-long LEAP program in Bangalore (official India page; Sep 2025 official post welcoming the campus cohort).
2. **Online assessments.** US NG (Aug 2025): two OAs, a Codility coding test and a HireVue video interview. US Master's-level role titled Staff Software Engineer (Aug 2026): 3 questions in 75 minutes, one 15-minute 'basic problem solving' question plus two 'intermediate problem solving' questions in 60 minutes. A May 2025 LeetCode report (location not stated): timed OA with DSA multiple-choice questions plus 2 basic coding problems. Israel junior roles: Codility test with 3 easy-medium questions taken on site with other candidates on a fixed day.
3. **Recruiter screen.** Behavioral questions plus logistics; candidates were not told it would be behavioral, so prepare stories.
4. **Onsite / final technical rounds.** US NG (Aug and Sep 2025, Santa Clara): 3 technical interviews, each with about 2 behavioral questions; discussion of resume projects, system design, and design-oriented coding rather than pure LeetCode; 'security-style' coding questions. A May 2025 LeetCode report (location not stated): Tech 1 DSA with complexity and alternative approaches; Tech 2 system design plus core CS (implement data structures, hashing and rehashing, polymorphism, browser internals, computer networks, OS).
5. **HR and offer.** Background check for finalists (official FAQ). US NG process took about 2 weeks in one Aug 2025 report.

### Intern

1. **Online assessment.** One OA round (US SDE intern, Apr 2025). A Sep 2025 LeetCode post says Technical Support Engineer internships also start with an online test.
2. **Recruiter call.** Background and logistics.
3. **Two technical interviews (about 1 hour each, Zoom).** Brief introduction then LeetCode-style questions, usually easy to medium for university interns (e.g. merge two sorted arrays of different lengths). Some behavioral questions ('a challenge working with a group').
4. **Decision.** US interns join a 12-week summer program; India interns often convert to full-time roles at the end of the internship (official India internship stories, 2025).

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

Experienced US loops (an Aug 2025 'Senior Software Engineer' report and a May 2025 'Software Engineer' report): recruiter screen, a hiring manager tech screen (projects, problem-solving approach, light coding and system design scenarios), then about three final rounds: coding with improvement follow-ups (a word ladder variant, then make it multithreaded; running code not required), a code improvement round with two engineers focused on multithreading (pseudocode allowed), and a real-world, work-related system design round. A Mar 2026 LeetCode post about the US 'Staff Software Engineer (Master's)' role says system design mostly focuses on networking and the loop has a code review round. Staff Software Engineer maps to about 2 to 3 yrs on Levels.fyi. Israel loops: HR call, a team lead interview on your stack (Aug 2025), then on-site coding (graph problems, an LC hard with the manager) and system design (Oct 2025). AI/ML and ML engineer loops (Sep 2025) add Python coding, ML concepts and networking or traffic-analysis scenarios. A US senior loop took about 4 months end to end (Apr 2025).

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** Codility (US NG 2025 and Israel) plus HireVue video (US NG 2025); the Aug 2026 US Master's-level OA used 'basic' and 'intermediate problem solving' sections (platform not named); India and apprentice OAs mix aptitude, MCQs and coding
- **Format:** US NG (2025): Codility coding test and a separate HireVue. US Master's 'Staff SWE' (Aug 2026): 3 questions in 75 minutes (15-minute basic section, 60-minute intermediate section with 2 problems). Israel: 3 easy to medium coding questions on site (sometimes a networking task such as building a socket in Python, or text-file processing in Python). Apprentice Engineer, Bengaluru (Jul 2025): aptitude (quant and logical reasoning), basic programming, output prediction or debugging, MCQs on OS, DBMS and DSA. Another 2025 report: DSA MCQs plus 2 basic coding problems.
- **Notes:** An Oct 2024 OA asked for the longest substring where every letter occurs an even number of times (prefix parity bitmask). Formats differ by country, degree level and role; you are told in advance if an assessment is required (official FAQ).

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** Intern: 2 technical. NG: 3 technical in the final loop (each mixes coding, design and behavioral). A 2025 LeetCode report (location not stated): 2 technical.
- **Style:** LeetCode easy to medium for interns and new grads (arrays, merging, tree traversal, DFS, backtracking, random shuffle), with some 'security-style' or design-oriented problems; hard questions appear in Israel manager rounds and for experienced hires.
- **Environment:** Zoom or Teams with a shared editor for remote rounds; Codility for OAs; Israel tests on your own laptop in their office. Running code was not required in at least one experienced round.
- **Graded on:** Correct and optimal code, complexity analysis, explaining alternatives and trade-offs, plus fundamentals (OOP, APIs, networking, OS).
- **Reported focus topics:** arrays and strings (LC easy to medium), sorting and merging, binary tree traversals, DFS and backtracking, randomization (shuffle), OOP and API fundamentals, hash table internals (hashing, rehashing), computer networks: TCP/IP, sockets, OSI model, firewalls, operating systems and multithreading, practical, security-flavored system design, troubleshooting scenarios (networking, Linux) and logic puzzles for India roles

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## Beyond LeetCode

HireVue video interview for US new grads (2025); on-site group Codility test days in Israel; networking tasks (sockets) inside OAs; core CS questions on computer networks, OS, DBMS and hashing (2025 reports); puzzle and troubleshooting questions in an India Python developer loop (12-coin weighing puzzle, 'connection refused' debugging, moving a 15 GB stream to 100 machines); code review or code improvement round focused on multithreading for experienced hires; networking-focused system design.

## System design

Can appear even for new grads: US NG loops in 2025 included system design discussion inside technical rounds, and a May 2025 report's Tech 2 combined system design with core CS. Flavor is practical and networking or security oriented (real work problems, networking-focused design for the US Staff (Master's) role) rather than consumer-scale product design. Experienced loops add a dedicated design round and a code review or code improvement round.

## Behavioral

**Framework:** Palo Alto Networks values (official culture page): Collaboration, Disruption, Execution, Inclusion, Integrity. A UK senior recruiter's official tips (Oct 2025) advise competency-based answers using STAR. ([official page](https://jobs.paloaltonetworks.com/en/culture))

**What they look for:**

- Knowledge of the company's products, customers and growth
- Adaptability and a growth mindset in a fast-changing security market
- Consultative, outcome-focused problem solving
- Collaboration across teams
- Clear STAR stories showing depth and breadth

**Questions to prepare:**

- Tell me about a time when you went above and beyond.
- Tell me about a challenge you had when working with a group.
- What do you do after making a mistake?
- Describe a feature you have developed.
- Walk me through the projects on your resume.

Build your answers with the [story bank](../behavioral/story-bank.md). Company detail: [behavioral guide](../behavioral/other-companies.md).

## Tips

- For US new grad roles, prepare for two separate OAs: a Codility coding test and a HireVue video interview.
- Treat the recruiter call as a behavioral interview; a 2025 new grad was not told it would be one.
- Revise computer networks (TCP/IP, sockets, OSI, firewalls) and OS basics; a May 2025 candidate who was strong in DSA was rejected for weak CN and OS.
- Practice LC easy-medium fundamentals (merge sorted arrays, tree traversals, DFS, shuffle) until you can also explain alternatives and complexity.
- Use STAR for competency questions and research the company's products, customers and growth (UK senior recruiter's official tips, Oct 2025); know the main platforms such as Cortex and Prisma and the Unit 42 threat-intel team.
- In India, target campus drives and internships: many interns convert to full time and new grads start in the LEAP program in Bangalore.
- With 2 to 3 years of experience, apply to 'Staff Software Engineer' roles; Levels.fyi shows Staff as the mid level. US Master's grads should not skip 'Staff Software Engineer (Master's)' postings: the title is not senior.
- For experienced loops, practice adding multithreading to an existing solution and reviewing someone else's code.
- For the Aug 2026 style OA, budget time: one short 'basic' problem in 15 minutes, then two harder problems in 60 minutes; read the requirements carefully because the basic problem is mostly multi-key sorting.
