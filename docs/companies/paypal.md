## Interview process

### New grad

1. **Application.** US: Recent Graduates Program (2 years, apply within 12 months of graduating, rolling year-round). India: campus placements plus job postings. Applications go through PayPal's Eightfold portal (paypal.eightfold.ai).
2. **Online assessment.** HackerRank. India Summer 2025 internship (2026 graduating batch, Dec 2024 report): 45 min, 1 DSA (greedy string with priority queue) + 5 CS MCQs. India Summer 2026 internship OA (Oct 30, 2025): 90 min. Experienced/early-career OAs mix 2 to 3 DSA with Spring Boot MCQs, SQL, or a machine-coding class design (Java library management system in 45 min, Jul 2025).
3. **Karat screen (US).** 60 min, recorded, run by a Karat interviewer. Reported format (iOS role Jun 2025; Senior role USA Jul 2025): about 5 min intro, 10 to 20 min reviewing or debugging code snippets for your domain (bugs, performance, OOP, clean code), then 30 to 45 min of 1 to 2 practical DSA problems that are not straight LeetCode. A new grad backend (Python) loop posted Nov 2024 (location not stated) started with Karat, then DSA and a Data Specialization round.
4. **DS/Algo round.** 45 to 60 min, LeetCode medium (DP and graphs common), working code, edge cases, complexity. India new grad/intern rounds also test resume and CS fundamentals.
5. **Role specialization round.** Domain or stack round: implement an LLD with given function signatures (booking system), language internals (Python multithreading vs multiprocessing, Spring Boot), frontend build tasks (React search bar with debouncing), or a 'Data Specialization' round for data-backend roles.
6. **System design.** Scenario-based, conversational. Usually for 2+ YOE, sometimes included for early-career.
7. **Bar Raiser / Leadership + hiring manager.** Behavioral round on leadership principles (ownership, cross-team collaboration, failure, conflict), then HM or team match. A US CL5 candidate (Aug 2025) listed 4 rounds: DSA, System Design, Leadership, Role Specialization. A backend 'virtual onsite' reported as 4 hours (Jan 2026, location not stated): algorithms coding, role specialization coding, system design, leadership. All virtual interviews run on Microsoft Teams (official page).

### Intern

1. **Application.** US/Canada: recruiting runs August through March, reviewed on a rolling basis; must return to school for at least one term after. India: application window July to December via campus placements; official process is 'online test and technical interview'.
2. **Online assessment.** HackerRank, 45 to 90 min, 1 to 2 DSA plus CS MCQs. India off-campus Summer 2026 OA (req R0130921, Bangalore) ran on Oct 30, 2025: one candidate reported 90 min, another solved both questions in 25 min.
3. **Technical interview(s).** India: Tech 1 and Tech 2 (DSA such as 2D-grid greedy and island/graph problems, resume, CS fundamentals), then an HM round. Off-campus Tech 1 rounds ran around Nov 28, 2025; candidates in Dec 2025 asked whether all rounds are eliminatory, unclear.
4. **HM / HR round.** 45 min with an engineering manager: experience, interests, projects (Dec 2024 report, India Summer 2025 internship).
5. **Conversion.** Interns can convert: a Jan 2026 India Software Engineer 1 offer came after a 3-month PayPal summer internship.

### With 1 to 3 years of experience

For 1 to 3 yrs (CL5/SWE 2) expect: HackerRank OA, DS/Algo round, System Design (scenario-based: ticket booking without double booking, notification service with idempotency, configuration/feature-flag service, flight booking), Role Specialization (LLD or stack depth: Spring Boot, concurrency, React), Bar Raiser, then HM/team matching. Some hires are 'pool hires' not tied to a team. Interviews can be cancelled or run poorly (interviewer unprepared, only theory questions) per 2025 to 2026 posts.

## Online assessment

- **Platform:** HackerRank (India and most experienced roles); Karat live screen for some US roles
- **Format:** Intern: 45 to 90 min, 1 to 2 DSA + CS MCQs. Early-career: 2 to 3 DSA (one easy, one hard), plus Spring Boot/Java MCQs, SQL, or a machine coding class design. SSE Python (Feb 2026): 4 questions covering SQL, DSA, machine coding
- **Notes:** Mixed sections mean time management decides the result; a candidate who skipped the 45-min library management design asked if they could still pass.

Prepare with [How to pass an OA](../online-assessments/strategy.md) and compare formats in [OA formats by company](../online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md).

## Coding rounds

- **Rounds:** 1 to 2 DS/Algo rounds plus a Role Specialization coding round
- **Style:** LeetCode medium, sometimes hard; DP, graphs, arrays, strings, heaps; practical Karat-style problems for US; LLD with predefined signatures in role specialization
- **Environment:** Microsoft Teams with HackerRank or CoderPad-style shared editor; Karat's own platform for Karat screens
- **Graded on:** Working code with edge cases, complexity, explaining choices, language and framework depth in the specialization round
- **Reported focus topics:** Dynamic programming, Graphs and grids (islands, BFS), Arrays and strings, sliding window, two pointers, Heaps and top-K, Monotonic stack, Concurrency and multithreading, LLD: booking, library, notification systems, Payments-flavored HLD: idempotency, double booking, feature flags, Java/Spring Boot or Python depth, SQL

Run every practice problem through [the 45-minute framework](../coding/interview-framework.md) and the [code quality rubric](../coding/code-quality.md).

## Beyond LeetCode

Role Specialization round (stack depth plus LLD with given signatures); Karat screen with a code review/debugging section before DSA; machine coding inside the HackerRank OA; frontend tasks (debounced search bar, React internals); 'Data Specialization' round for data-backend roles.

## System design

Interns and new grads: usually none, resume-based design questions possible. 2+ YOE: a dedicated, conversational HLD round with payments-flavored scenarios (idempotency, double booking, real-time updates, configuration rollout and rollback). LLD/machine coding appears in OAs and role specialization (library management, booking system, concurrency control, notification system).

Start with [who needs system design](../system-design/index.md), then the [framework](../system-design/framework.md).

## Behavioral

**Framework:** Values (Inclusion, Innovation, Collaboration, Wellness) plus Leadership Principles (Put People First, Work Customer Back, Win Together); assessed in a 'Bar Raiser' or leadership round ([official page](https://about.pypl.com/who-we-are/mission-vision-values/default.aspx))

**What they look for:**

- Specific, detailed examples per competency (official tip)
- Examples from more than one job or project (official: 'Vary your examples')
- Ownership and handling failure
- Cross-team collaboration and resolving architectural disagreements
- Customer-first reasoning (Work Customer Back)

**Questions to prepare:**

- Tell me about a time you owned a project end to end.
- Describe a failure and what you changed afterward.
- Tell me about a disagreement with another team on an architectural decision.
- How have you collaborated across teams to ship something?
- Why are you looking to switch, and why PayPal?

Build your answers with the [story bank](../behavioral/story-bank.md). Company detail: [behavioral guide](../behavioral/other-companies.md).

## Tips

- Practice mixed OAs: solve 2 DSA problems and one small class design in 60 to 90 min, since PayPal's HackerRank tests mix sections.
- For US roles, rehearse a Karat-style hour: read and debug code snippets out loud for 15 minutes, then solve 1 to 2 practical problems.
- Pick one stack and go deep for the Role Specialization round: Spring Boot internals, Python concurrency, or React performance.
- Prepare payment-domain design answers: idempotency keys, exactly-once processing, double booking prevention, retries.
- Map 6 to 8 STAR stories to Put People First, Work Customer Back, and Win Together; PayPal's own interview page tells you to research its Leadership Principles and vary your examples.
- Keep other processes running after the HM round; several Q4 2025 candidates waited weeks for offer letters or were told the HM wanted more candidates.
- India campus candidates: revise OS, DBMS, OOP, and networks for the OA MCQs and the first technical round.
- First-generation US or India students can apply to Career Academy (tech applications Nov 4 to 28), which can lead to PayPal interviews.

## 4-week plan for PayPal

Do this after you finish a core list like [Grind 75 or NeetCode 150](../coding/problem-lists.md).

- [ ] Week 1: Solve problems 1 to 20 from the most frequent list. Time-box each at 30 minutes.
- [ ] Week 2: Solve problems 21 to 40. Re-solve any you failed in week 1 without looking.
- [ ] Week 3: Solve the signature problems and every reported question above. Do 2 timed mock interviews.
- [ ] Week 4: Write 8 stories for the Values (Inclusion, Innovation, Collaboration, Wellness) plus Leadership Principles (Put People First, Work Customer Back, Win Together); assessed in a 'Bar Raiser' or leadership round round. Do 2 full mock loops. Review the online assessment and system design notes above.
