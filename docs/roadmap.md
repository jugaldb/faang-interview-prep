## Setup

Pick a plan, a role and a language. Set up your logs.

- [ ] **Read Start here and pick a 4, 8, 12 or 16 week plan.** Choose the plan that fits your free hours per week. Each plan says what to do in which week. [Start here](start-here.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Read one complete job search plan before you start.** Interns and new grads: read the internship and new grad prep post. Experienced: read the 6-week roadmap. [Ascend: The New Grad and Internship prep for 2026](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-new-grad-and-internship-prep), [Ascend: The 6-week job search roadmap](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-only-6-week-job-search-roadmap). 1 h.
- [ ] **Write down your target role and level at each company.** Pick one role family (SWE, AI engineer, SRE, data, quant dev) and the matching level, for example L3 at Google or SDE I at Amazon. [What jobs to apply for](jobs/index.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Put the Summer 2027 recruiting dates in your calendar.** Internships are reviewed on a rolling basis and many open in late summer. Copy the months into your calendar with reminders. [How internship recruiting works](internships/index.md), [Simplify: Summer 2027 internship timeline](https://simplify.jobs/blog/summer-2027-internship-timeline), [Ascend: 494 Summer 2027 internships are already live](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/494-summer-2027-internships-are-already). 0.5 h. _Intern only._
- [ ] **Check your visa options if you need sponsorship.** Learn what CPT, OPT and STEM OPT allow. Check a company's H-1B history before you spend time tailoring for it. [International students](jobs/international-students.md), [Ascend: How to check if a company sponsors H-1B visas](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-check-if-a-company-sponsors), [USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub](https://www.uscis.gov/tools/reports-and-studies/h-1b-employer-data-hub). 1 h.
- [ ] **Block a fixed daily study slot for the whole timeline.** The same slot every day beats long weekend sessions. Size it from the weekly hours in your plan. [Start here](start-here.md). 0.25 h.
- [ ] **Pick one coding language for every interview.** Use the language you write fastest. Learn its built-in lists, maps, sets, heaps and sorting cold. [How to learn DSA](coding/index.md), [NeetCode: Python for coding interviews](https://neetcode.io/courses/lessons/python-for-coding-interviews). 1 h.
- [ ] **Create LeetCode and NeetCode accounts and a problem log.** Log every problem: date, pattern, minutes taken, whether you needed help, and a date to redo it. [How to practice](coding/how-to-practice.md), [NeetCode roadmap](https://neetcode.io/roadmap), [LeetCode study plans](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Bookmark the Ascend post index and the tools list.** Every Ascend post is mapped to the section it supports. Use both when a task needs more depth. [Ascend posts by topic](resources/substack.md), [Tools list](resources/tools.md), [Ascend newsletter](https://jugaldb.substack.com). 0.25 h.

Resume v1, LinkedIn, a job list and DSA basics.

- [ ] **Draft resume v1 in one sitting.** Fill every section, then cut to one page. To start fast, turn your LinkedIn profile into a LaTeX resume with the prompt in the post. [Resume rules](resume/index.md), [Ascend: From LinkedIn to ATS resume in 1 minute](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/from-linkedin-to-ats-resume-in-1). 3 h.

[Writing bullets](resume/writing-bullets.md), [Ascend: How to become an AI engineer in 2026](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-become-an-ai-engineer-in-2026). 3 h. _Intern and new grad._

[Resume checklist](resume/checklist.md), [Ascend: How I turned my resume into a job magnet](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-i-turned-my-resume-into-a-job). 1 h.
- [ ] **Set a clear LinkedIn photo and banner.** Face visible, plain background, good light. An AI headshot made from your own photo is fine if it still looks like you. [LinkedIn profile steps](linkedin/index.md), [Ascend: A professional LinkedIn photo with AI](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/ai-tool-gave-me-professional-linkedin). 0.5 h.

- [ ] **Copy your resume bullets into LinkedIn experience.** Add the skills your target job descriptions ask for. Set a custom profile URL with your name. [LinkedIn profile steps](linkedin/index.md), [LinkedIn Step By Step (Jugal's Notion guide)](https://jugaldb.notion.site/LinkedIn-Step-By-Step-by-Jugal-Bhatt-265af2117b838071a066e4db145acf57). 1 h.

[Open to Work settings](linkedin/index.md#step-15-open-to-work-recruiters-only), [How recruiters search](linkedin/recruiter-search.md). 0.25 h.
- [ ] **Watch the Summer 2027 internships list on GitHub.** Watch the repo or turn on email alerts. If you need sponsorship, skip rows marked no sponsorship or US citizenship required. [Finding and applying](internships/finding-and-applying.md), [SimplifyJobs Summer 2027 Internships](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships), [SWEList email alerts](https://swelist.com/). 0.5 h. _Intern only._
- [ ] **Watch the New Grad Positions list on GitHub.** Watch the repo or turn on email alerts. If you need sponsorship, skip rows marked no sponsorship or US citizenship required. [Where to find jobs](jobs/where-to-find-jobs.md), [SimplifyJobs New Grad Positions](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions), [SWEList email alerts](https://swelist.com/). 0.5 h. _New grad only._
- [ ] **Set job alerts on 3 sources and check them daily.** Use company career pages, one job board and LinkedIn alerts. Daily checks let you apply in the first days a role is live. [Where to find jobs](jobs/where-to-find-jobs.md), [Ascend: Stop applying to ghost jobs](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/stop-applying-to-ghost-jobs). 1 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Build a target list of 30 companies in 3 tiers.** Tier 1: dream companies. Tier 2: strong fits. Tier 3: companies likely to say yes, including ones outside big tech. [Company guides](companies/index.md), [Application strategy](jobs/application-strategy.md). 1.5 h.
- [ ] **Check early-career programs you qualify for.** Some programs target first and second-year students or specific groups. Put each deadline in your tracker. [Programs by company](internships/programs.md). 1 h. _Intern only._
- [ ] **Read how to learn DSA and set a daily problem count.** Pick a number of problems per day you can keep for the whole timeline. Learn a topic, then practice it the same day. [How to learn DSA](coding/index.md). 0.5 h.

[Topics and complexity](coding/topics.md), [Tech Interview Handbook: study cheatsheet](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/study-cheatsheet/). 2 h.
- [ ] **Learn arrays, strings and hash maps.** Learn the operations and their costs. Then solve 5 easy problems on each. [Topics and complexity](coding/topics.md), [Tech Interview Handbook: Array](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/array/), [Tech Interview Handbook: Hash table](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/hash-table/). 6 h. _Intern and new grad._
- [ ] **Learn linked lists, stacks and queues.** Implement each one yourself once. Then solve 3 easy problems on each. [Topics and complexity](coding/topics.md), [Tech Interview Handbook: Linked list](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/linked-list/), [Tech Interview Handbook: Stack](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/stack/). 4 h. _Intern and new grad._
- [ ] **Learn recursion, trees and binary search.** Trace recursive calls on paper until they feel normal. Then write tree traversals from memory. [Topics and complexity](coding/topics.md), [Tech Interview Handbook: Recursion](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/recursion/), [Tech Interview Handbook: Tree](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/tree/), [VisuAlgo: BST](https://visualgo.net/en/bst). 6 h. _Intern and new grad._
- [ ] **Refresh the basics with one easy problem per data structure.** Arrays, hash maps, linked lists, stacks, queues and trees. If one feels slow, redo that topic in full. [Topics and complexity](coding/topics.md), [LeetCode 75 study plan](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/leetcode-75/). 4 h. _Experienced only._

## Core DSA

One main problem list, pattern by pattern.

- [ ] **Pick one main problem list and finish it in order.** For example Grind 75 or NeetCode 150. Switching lists halfway costs you time. [Problem lists](coding/problem-lists.md), [Grind 75](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/), [NeetCode roadmap](https://neetcode.io/roadmap). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Use a fixed per-problem process with a time limit.** Give each problem 20 to 30 minutes before you open a solution. Write the pattern in your log. [How to practice](coding/how-to-practice.md), [Ascend: I cleared Amazon, Google and Meta with only 120 LeetCode problems](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/i-cleared-amazon-google-and-meta). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Read the core patterns page once.** Learn the signal that points to each pattern. Come back to it after every problem. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md), [Sean Prashad's LeetCode Patterns](https://seanprashad.com/leetcode-patterns/). 1 h.
- [ ] **Solve the two pointers and sliding window problems.** Sorted input, pairs, or a contiguous subarray with a condition point here. Do every one on your main list. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md). 8 h.
- [ ] **Solve the binary search problems.** Include binary search on the answer: the smallest speed, capacity or time that works. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md), [LeetCode Binary Search study plan](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/binary-search/). 5 h.
- [ ] **Solve the stack and linked list problems.** Matching brackets, next greater element, fast and slow pointers, reversal and merge. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md). 6 h.
- [ ] **Solve the tree BFS and DFS problems.** BFS for levels, DFS for paths and subtrees, and BST order for search. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md). 8 h.
- [ ] **Solve the heap and top K problems.** Top K elements, K-way merge and running median. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md). 4 h.
- [ ] **Solve the graph problems: BFS, DFS, topological sort.** Treat grids as graphs. Add union-find for connectivity questions. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md), [LeetCode Graph Theory study plan](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/graph-theory/). 10 h.
- [ ] **Solve the backtracking problems.** Subsets, permutations and combinations. Draw the decision tree before you code. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md). 5 h.
- [ ] **Solve the dynamic programming problems, 1D then 2D.** Start with climbing stairs and house robber, then grids and two-string problems. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md), [LeetCode Dynamic Programming study plan](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/dynamic-programming/). 12 h.
- [ ] **Solve the intervals and greedy problems.** Sort by start or end time, then sweep once. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md). 4 h.
- [ ] **Solve the trie and bit manipulation problems.** Tries for prefix search and autocomplete. Bit tricks for single number and subset questions. [Coding patterns](coding/patterns.md), [Tech Interview Handbook: Trie](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/trie/). 3 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Redo 10 problems from your log without notes.** Pick the ones you needed help on. If you get stuck again, schedule it for next week. [How to practice](coding/how-to-practice.md). 5 h.
- [ ] **Solve 10 medium problems in under 25 minutes each.** Time yourself and talk out loud as if an interviewer is listening. [How to practice](coding/how-to-practice.md). 5 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Learn what interviewers grade in your code.** Clear names, small helpers and your own test cases are graded too. A passing answer is not enough. [Code quality](coding/code-quality.md). 1 h.

## Applications and outreach

Tailored applications, referrals, recruiters, cold email and a tracker.

- [ ] **Read the funnel math and the referral-first order.** A referral beats recruiter outreach, which beats a cold application. Plan each week around that order. [Application strategy](jobs/application-strategy.md), [Ascend: How I increase my chances of getting interview callbacks](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-i-increase-my-chances-of-getting). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Read the process for each target company.** Online assessment, rounds and timeline. Note anything that differs from this plan. [Company guides](companies/index.md).
- [ ] **Set up your application and outreach tracker.** One row per application: company, role, link, date, referral, status and next follow-up date. [Follow-up and tracking](outreach/follow-up-and-tracking.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Learn the tailoring routine for one job description.** Map the job's keywords to your bullets, then rewrite the top third of the page. The post has the exact prompts. [Resume tailoring](resume/tailoring.md), [Ascend: I asked Claude to make my resume unrejectable](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/i-asked-claude-to-make-my-resume). 1 h.
- [ ] **Find roles posted in the last 24 hours with Google search.** Search ATS domains with site: and a date filter every weekday morning. [Where to find jobs](jobs/where-to-find-jobs.md), [Ascend: Stop applying to ghost jobs](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/stop-applying-to-ghost-jobs). 1 h.
- [ ] **Apply to each new internship within 48 hours of posting.** Many SWE internships stay open only a few days. Keep a ready resume so each application takes minutes. [Finding and applying](internships/finding-and-applying.md), [Ascend: 494 Summer 2027 internships are already live](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/494-summer-2027-internships-are-already). 6 h. _Intern only._
- [ ] **Tailor and send your first 10 applications.** Start with tier 2 and tier 3 companies so you get practice before your top picks. [Resume tailoring](resume/tailoring.md), [Application strategy](jobs/application-strategy.md). 5 h.
- [ ] **Send a tailored batch of applications every week.** Pick a fixed weekly number and log each one. Tick this when the weekly habit is running. [Application strategy](jobs/application-strategy.md), [Ascend: 7 videos to run your entire job search with AI](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-to-run-your-entire-job-search). 10 h.
- [ ] **Find a recruiter and 2 engineers at each tier 1 company.** Use LinkedIn search filters and your alumni network. Save names and profile links in the tracker. [Finding people and emails](outreach/finding-people.md), [The Holy Grail of Networking (Jugal's Notion guide)](https://jugaldb.notion.site/The-Holy-Grail-of-Networking-A-Z-with-templates-1a0af2117b838027aa5cd47911f2a20f). 3 h.
- [ ] **Find and verify work emails for your contacts.** Use an email finder, then verify each address before you send. Free tiers cover a small weekly list. [Finding people and emails](outreach/finding-people.md), [Hunter (freemium)](https://hunter.io/). 1 h.
- [ ] **Send 10 LinkedIn connection notes a week.** Short, specific notes to alumni and engineers on teams you want. Ask for a 15-minute chat, not a job. [Message templates](outreach/templates.md), [Ascend: 7 videos on networking your way to offers](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-on-networking-your-way-to). 2 h.
- [ ] **Ask for referrals after a real conversation.** Send the job link, your resume and 2 lines on why you fit. Make it easy for the referrer to submit. [Referrals](outreach/referrals.md), [Message templates](outreach/templates.md), [Ascend: The job hunt I didn't burn out doing](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-job-hunt-i-didnt-burn-out-doing). 2 h.
- [ ] **Message recruiters at your tier 1 and tier 2 companies.** One short message per recruiter with the role ID and your strongest proof point. [Outreach strategy](outreach/index.md), [Message templates](outreach/templates.md). 2 h.
- [ ] **Send cold emails to hiring managers.** Use the subject lines and send times on the cold email page. Log every reply. [Cold email](outreach/cold-email.md), [Ascend: The one skill that can unlock every opportunity](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-one-skill-that-can-unlock-every). 3 h.
- [ ] **Follow up after 5 to 7 business days, at most twice.** Add something new in each follow-up: a project, a result or a question. Then stop. [Follow-up and tracking](outreach/follow-up-and-tracking.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Automate referral emails with the n8n Referral Engine (optional).** It reads contacts from a sheet, finds emails with Hunter, drafts with Gemini and sends through Gmail. [n8n automation](outreach/n8n-automation.md), [Ascend: The Referral Engine (n8n, Hunter, Gemini, Gmail)](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-referral-engine-n8n-hunter-gemini). 4 h.

[n8n automation](outreach/n8n-automation.md), [Ascend: Ultimate Job Search Workflow with n8n](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/ultimate-job-search-workflow-with). 3 h.
- [ ] **Write and rehearse a 30-second pitch.** Hook, who you are, what you built, why this company, then the ask. Practice until it sounds like talking. [Career fairs](outreach/career-fairs.md), [Ascend: Craft the elevator pitch that gets you hired](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/craft-the-elevator-pitch-that-gets). 1.5 h. _Intern and new grad._
- [ ] **Attend one career fair or tech event and follow up in 24 hours.** Bring your pitch and resume. Write down each recruiter's name and email for the follow-up. [Career fairs](outreach/career-fairs.md). 3 h. _Intern and new grad._
- [ ] **Add the keywords recruiters search for to your profile.** Recruiters filter by title, skills and location. Use the same words as your target job descriptions. [How recruiters search](linkedin/recruiter-search.md). 1 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Post on LinkedIn once a week (optional).** Share what you built or learned. Comment on posts by engineers at your target companies. [Posting for inbound](linkedin/content.md), [Ascend: How I got 47 recruiter messages within a month](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-i-got-47-recruiter-messages-within). 2 h.
- [ ] **Add a few startups to your list for faster processes.** Early startups still read applications and can move in weeks. Jugal got his first startup offer in 17 days. [Ascend: How I got my first startup offer in 17 days](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-i-got-my-first-startup-offer), [Wellfound jobs](https://wellfound.com/jobs). 1 h. _New grad and experienced._

## Online assessments

Formats, platforms and timed practice.

- [ ] **Learn how online assessments are scored.** Most score hidden test cases within a time limit. Some also review your code and check for plagiarism. [How OAs work](online-assessments/index.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Look up the OA format for each tier 1 company.** Platform, number of questions, time limit and extra sections such as work-style surveys. [OA formats by company](online-assessments/company-oa-formats.md), [Company guides](companies/index.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Practice on the platform your target companies use.** Get used to the editor, the input format and running your own tests. [OA platforms](online-assessments/platforms.md), [HackerRank Interview Preparation Kit](https://www.hackerrank.com/interview/interview-preparation-kit), [CodeSignal Learn (freemium)](https://codesignal.com/learn). 2 h.
- [ ] **Take 3 timed practice OAs, one sitting each.** Same time limit, no notes, camera on if the real one needs it. Review every failed test case after. [How to pass OAs](online-assessments/strategy.md). 5 h.
- [ ] **Prepare your OA day: quiet room, charger, rules read.** Read the integrity rules first. A partial solution that passes some tests beats an empty one. [How to pass OAs](online-assessments/strategy.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Write OA code that a reviewer can read.** Some companies read your OA code after the tests pass. Use clear names and delete dead code. [Code quality in OAs](online-assessments/code-quality.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Practice the non-coding OA sections.** Work-style surveys, work simulations and game-based tests. Answer consistently and honestly. [OA platforms](online-assessments/platforms.md), [Amazon: SDE online assessment](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/university/sde-oa). 1 h.

## Interview prep

Mocks, company lists, your story bank and design rounds.

- [ ] **Practice the 45-minute coding interview framework out loud.** Clarify, examples, approach, code, test, complexity. Say each step before you do it. [The 45-minute interview](coding/interview-framework.md). 3 h.
- [ ] **Open the guides for your top 3 companies.** Note what each guide says about the online assessment, the rounds and the values they test. [Company guides](companies/index.md), [Company-wise DSA patterns (Jugal's Notion guide)](https://jugaldb.notion.site/Company-Wise-DSA-patterns-26caf2117b83808eb7b2efae6afd15dc), [Company-wise LeetCode lists (GitHub)](https://github.com/liquidslr/leetcode-company-wise-problems). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Read Jugal's prep post for each FAANG company you target.** Each post lists the topics that company favors and a 5-week plan. [Ascend: Meta (Part 1)](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-crack-faang-interviews-part), [Ascend: Amazon (Part 2)](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-crack-faang-interviews-part-7f8), [Ascend: Google (Part 3)](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-crack-faang-interviews-part-e6e), [Ascend: Apple (Part 4)](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-crack-faang-interviews-part-8c8), [Ascend: Netflix (Part 5)](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-crack-faang-interviews-part-020). 1 h.
- [ ] **Solve the top 20 problems for each target company.** Work down the most frequent problems list on each guide. Give each problem 20 to 30 minutes before you open a solution. [Company guides](companies/index.md).
- [ ] **Do 3 peer mock interviews.** Swap interviewer and candidate roles. Ask for feedback on how you communicate as well as on correctness. [Mock interviews](coding/mock-interviews.md), [Aced practice (free peer mocks)](https://www.aced.io/practice). 4.5 h.
- [ ] **Do 1 AI mock interview and review the transcript.** Look for long silences, skipped clarifying questions and missing complexity analysis. [Mock interviews](coding/mock-interviews.md), [interviewing.io (free AI interviewer)](https://interviewing.io/). 1 h.
- [ ] **Prepare for coding rounds that use an AI assistant.** Some loops now let you code with AI. Practice reading, testing and explaining code you did not write. [Ascend: How to prepare for FAANG AI engineer internship season](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-prepare-for-faang-ai-engineer), [Aced: Google AI coding interview](https://www.aced.io/blog/google-ai-coding-interview). 1.5 h.
- [ ] **Learn how behavioral rounds are scored.** Answer with STAR: situation, task, action, result. Most of the answer should be your actions. [Behavioral interviews](behavioral/index.md), [Tech Interview Handbook: Behavioral interview](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview/). 1 h.
- [ ] **Write a story bank of 8 to 10 stories.** Conflict, failure, ambiguity, ownership, a tight deadline, helping a teammate. Interns can use projects, clubs and part-time jobs. [Story bank](behavioral/story-bank.md), [Ascend: How to prepare for behavioral interviews](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-to-prepare-for-behavioral-interviews). 4 h.
- [ ] **Map each story to the most common questions.** Every common question should point to at least one story. Write a new story for each gap. [Story bank](behavioral/story-bank.md). 2 h.
- [ ] **Prepare stories for each target company's values.** Map your story bank to what each company looks for. Rehearse each answer out loud. [Amazon Leadership Principles](behavioral/amazon-leadership-principles.md), [Google](behavioral/google-googleyness.md), [Meta](behavioral/meta.md), [Microsoft](behavioral/microsoft.md), [Apple](behavioral/apple.md), [Netflix](behavioral/netflix.md), [Other companies](behavioral/other-companies.md).
- [ ] **Rehearse each story out loud in about 2 minutes.** Record yourself once. Cut the background and end on a result with a number. [Behavioral interviews](behavioral/index.md). 2 h.
- [ ] **Write a why-this-company answer for each tier 1 company.** 3 sentences: the product, the problem you want to work on, and proof you can help. [Story bank](behavioral/story-bank.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Prepare to explain every line on your resume.** For each project: the problem, your design choices, what broke and what you would change. [Resume rules](resume/index.md). 1.5 h.
- [ ] **Read what intern interviews look like.** Intern loops are usually shorter than new grad loops. Check the format at each target company. [Intern interviews](internships/intern-interviews.md). 0.5 h. _Intern only._
- [ ] **Check if your target loops include system design.** Most new grad loops do not. Some include a low-level design round instead. [System design: who gets it](system-design/index.md). 0.5 h. _New grad only._
- [ ] **Practice 5 low-level design problems.** Parking lot, LRU cache, rate limiter, elevator, vending machine. Classes, interfaces and one design pattern each. [Low-level design](system-design/low-level-design.md), [Awesome Low-Level Design (GitHub)](https://github.com/ashishps1/awesome-low-level-design). 6 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Learn the system design fundamentals.** Caching, databases, sharding, queues, consistency and load balancing, one at a time. [System design fundamentals](system-design/fundamentals.md), [System Design Primer (GitHub)](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer), [Hello Interview: System design in a hurry](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/in-a-hurry/introduction). 10 h. _Experienced only._
- [ ] **Learn the system design interview framework.** Requirements, estimates, API, data model, high-level design, deep dives. Practice the time boxes. [System design framework](system-design/framework.md). 2 h. _Experienced only._
- [ ] **Design 6 classic systems out loud.** URL shortener, rate limiter, news feed, chat, file storage, and one product from your target company. [Classic design problems](system-design/problems.md), [Hello Interview: Bitly breakdown](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/problem-breakdowns/bitly). 12 h. _Experienced only._
- [ ] **Do 2 system design mock interviews.** Ask the mock interviewer to push on scale and failure cases. [Mock interviews](coding/mock-interviews.md), [System design resources](system-design/resources.md). 3 h. _Experienced only._
- [ ] **Check the common reasons strong candidates fail.** Compare the list with your mock feedback and fix the top 2 gaps. [Ascend: Why smart candidates still fail FAANG interviews](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/why-smart-candidates-still-fail-faang). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Redo 15 problems you marked as weak.** Do this in the last week before your first onsite. Start no new topics that week. [How to practice](coding/how-to-practice.md). 6 h.

## Interview loop

Day-of routine, notes, thank-you and follow-up.

- [ ] **Schedule rounds with a few days of buffer.** Ask the recruiter what each round covers and pick dates that leave you time to review. [Message templates](outreach/templates.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Test your camera, mic, editor and internet the day before.** Use the exact tool the invite names. Keep a phone hotspot as a backup. [The 45-minute interview](coding/interview-framework.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Review your story bank and 10 marked problems the day before.** Read the stories out loud once. Re-read your notes on each problem; do not start new ones. [Story bank](behavioral/story-bank.md), [How to practice](coding/how-to-practice.md). 2 h.
- [ ] **Warm up with one easy problem on interview day.** Then stop studying. Keep water and a notepad within reach. [The 45-minute interview](coding/interview-framework.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Prepare 2 questions to ask each interviewer.** Ask about the team's current work, how success is measured and what new hires find hard. [Behavioral interviews](behavioral/index.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Write notes within an hour of each round.** Questions asked, what went well, what to fix. Use them before the next round or loop. [Follow-up and tracking](outreach/follow-up-and-tracking.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Send thank-you notes within 24 hours.** Keep it short and mention one specific detail. Send it through the recruiter if you lack interviewer emails. [Message templates](outreach/templates.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Follow up with the recruiter if the reply date passes.** One polite email asking for an update. Keep your other processes moving meanwhile. [Follow-up and tracking](outreach/follow-up-and-tracking.md), [Message templates](outreach/templates.md). 0.25 h.
- [ ] **Prepare for team matching calls.** Some companies, such as Google and Meta, match you to a team after you pass the interviews. Prepare questions and say what you want to build. [Google guide](companies/google.md), [Meta guide](companies/meta.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **If rejected, ask for feedback and the reapply window.** Log what to fix, then go back to the phase that covers it. [Application strategy](jobs/application-strategy.md). 0.5 h.

## Offer and negotiation

Comp basics, scripts and comparing offers.

- [ ] **Read the offer process from recruiter call to signature.** Know every step before the first recruiter call, because the salary question can come early. [Negotiation process](negotiation/index.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Prepare your answer to the salary expectations question.** Avoid naming a number before you have an offer. Use the script word for word. [Negotiation scripts](negotiation/scripts.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Learn base, bonus, stock, vesting and sign-on.** Interns: compare hourly pay, housing and relocation instead. [Comp basics](negotiation/comp-basics.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Look up real pay data for your level and city.** Compare by level, not title. Save 3 data points to use on the call. [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/), [Negotiation resources](negotiation/resources.md). 1 h.
- [ ] **Line up other processes so offers land close together.** Ask each recruiter for their timeline. Tell slower companies when you have an offer deadline. [Negotiation process](negotiation/index.md), [Application strategy](jobs/application-strategy.md). 1 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Ask for more with the negotiation email script.** Thank them, state a number with a reason, and ask what is possible. [Negotiation scripts](negotiation/scripts.md), [Haseeb Qureshi: Ten rules for negotiating a job offer](https://haseebq.com/my-ten-rules-for-negotiating-a-job-offer/). 1 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Ask for an extension if a deadline is too short.** Ask early and propose a date. Short deadlines can often be moved. [Negotiation scripts](negotiation/scripts.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Compare offers on total pay over 4 years.** Include the vesting schedule, sign-on, refreshers if known, and cost of living. [Comp basics](negotiation/comp-basics.md). 1 h. _New grad and experienced._
- [ ] **Confirm sponsorship, start date and location in writing.** International students: confirm the company supports STEM OPT and will file for H-1B before you sign. [International students](jobs/international-students.md). 0.5 h.
- [ ] **Plan for a return offer from your first week.** Set goals with your manager in week one and ask for feedback at the midpoint. [How internship recruiting works](internships/index.md), [Simplify: How to get a return offer](https://simplify.jobs/blog/how-to-get-a-return-offer-during-your-internship/). 0.5 h. _Intern only._
- [ ] **Sign, withdraw elsewhere and thank everyone who helped.** Tell the other companies politely. Send referrers and mentors a short thank-you. [Message templates](outreach/templates.md). 0.5 h.
