# What jobs to apply for

For students, new grads and engineers with 0 to 3 years of experience. Use it to pick 2 role families, your target level at each company, and the title keywords you will search with.

What this means for you:

- Each big company has fewer new grad seats than in 2019. Search more title variants and more companies than the famous five.
- AI and ML roles now outnumber classic SWE roles in internship lists. Both loops still test DSA.
- Budget about 3 months from first application to first offer (the Q2 2026 median was 82 days). Start applying 3 months before you need an offer.

## Title keywords to search

Searching only "new grad" misses more than half the market. Of 295 open new grad SWE listings in the [Simplify New Grad list](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions) on Oct 4, 2026, titles used: "New Grad" 128, "Engineer I" or "1" 91, "Junior" 34, "Early Career" 23, "Entry Level" 18, "University Grad" or "College Grad" about 5. Titles overlap.

Copy this list into your search tools:

```text
"new grad" OR "new graduate" OR "university grad" OR "college grad" OR "early career"
OR "entry level" OR "junior" OR "engineer I" OR "engineer 1" OR "associate software engineer"
OR "2026 start" OR "2027 start" OR "AMTS" OR "SDE I"
Add if it fits you: "forward deployed", "machine learning engineer", "AI engineer", "Master's" (MS-only tracks)
```

> **Tip:** Master's students should search for "Master's" in the title before the general pool. Capital One and NVIDIA run separate advanced-degree tracks with smaller applicant pools ([Jugal's post](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/494-summer-2027-internships-are-already)).

## Which level to aim for

| Your profile | Apply mainly to | Stretch | Usually not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| BS, 0 internships | Entry-level SWE at mid-size companies, Engineer I, Data Engineer I, startups, Leap-style programs | Big tech new grad SWE | Research scientist, quant research |
| BS or MS, 1 to 2 internships | Big tech new grad (L3, E3, SDE I, 59 to 60), FDE new grad, quant dev | AI or ML engineer new grad | Research scientist |
| MS with ML projects or a paper | ML engineer new grad, AI engineer, MS-level applied scientist, FDE | Research engineer | PhD-only research roles |
| PhD | Research scientist, research engineer, PhD early-career SWE | | |
| 1 to 3 years of experience | Next level up (L4, E4, SDE II, 61 to 62, ICT3) at strong companies | Senior titles | |

Rules that hold across companies:

1. Apply at the entry level until you have about 2 years of full-time experience. Levels.fyi data for Google L4 starts at about 2 years.
2. With 1 to 3 years, apply one level up. Accept the entry level at a stronger company if you are switching domains.
3. Expect some companies to set your level after the loop. A strong loop can move you up, a weak one down.
4. Check the promotion pace before you sign. Meta expects E3 to E4 in about 2 years, and Google L3 to L4 averages over 2 years ([Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/choosing-between-companies/)).

## How to read a job description

Do this for every role you plan to tailor a resume for. It takes 5 minutes.

1. **Read the title.** New Grad, University Grad, Early Career, I, Junior, or "[year] start" means entry level. "Senior", "Staff" or "III" usually does not. Exceptions are in the level table: Walmart's entry title is Software Engineer II, and Palo Alto Networks uses "Staff" at about 2 to 3 years.
2. **Note the posting date and req ID.** Find the same req on the company's own site. If it is not there, run the [60-second ghost-job check](where-to-find-jobs.md#60-second-ghost-job-check).
3. **Split minimum from preferred.** Minimums are filters, preferred items are tie-breakers. Google's campus posting accepts DSA experience "during coursework/projects, research, internships" ([2025 copy](https://jobs.anitab.org/companies/google-24698/jobs/45702718-software-engineer-early-career-campus)), so coursework counts there.
4. **Translate the experience line.** "0 to 2 years" or "1 year" is reachable with internships and projects (Google's "Software Engineer II" postings ask for 1 year). "3+ years" is not a new grad role.
5. **Check the degree and graduation window.** "Currently pursuing or completed" means students can apply. Note the window, for example "Dec 2026 to Jun 2027".
6. **Check the start date.** "Availability to start in 2027" means this is the new grad cycle for 2027 grads.
7. **Check location and office policy.** Remote-only postings got 3.53% interviews per application vs 6.07% for hybrid in Q2 2026 ([Huntr](https://huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q2-2026)).
8. **Ctrl+F for visa words.** Search "sponsor", "export control", "US person", "citizen", "clearance", "ITAR". Defense and aerospace roles usually need citizenship or a green card ([Jugal's F-1 check](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/494-summer-2027-internships-are-already)).
9. **Read the pay range.** Several US states and Ontario require one. Use it to sanity-check the level.
10. **Pull 5 to 8 exact terms you have.** Put them in your resume word for word. Never add a skill you lack ([Resume tailoring](../resume/tailoring.md)).

Worked example: Amazon's [SDE 2026 (US) posting](https://amazon.jobs/en/jobs/3177934/software-development-engineer-2026-us).

| JD line | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| One general-purpose language; data structures, algorithms and/or OOD | Classic DSA loop. Prepare with [Coding patterns](../coding/patterns.md) |
| Currently pursuing or holding a bachelor's in CS or related STEM | Students can apply before graduating |
| Preferred: internships or projects, AI dev tools, AWS, SQL/NoSQL, open source | Tie-breakers. Mirror the ones you have |
| "Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis" | Apply in week one. Later applicants compete for fewer slots |
| Base $110,500 to $160,000 (Seattle reference) | Entry level (SDE I, L4) |

New in 2026: Amazon's [Summer 2027 SDE intern posting](https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10552937/software-development-engineer-intern-summer-2027-usa) lists "Experience using AI-assisted development tools" as a basic qualification. Put honest AI tool use on your resume, including how you checked the output.

Decision rule after reading:

| You have | Do this |
|---|---|
| All minimums, most preferred | Apply in week one, tailor fully, look for a referral |
| All minimums, few preferred | Apply, tailor the skills line and top 3 bullets |
| Missing only a "years" minimum, but you have internships | Apply if the title is entry level (Uber's L3, for example, typically has 1 to 2 years) |
| Missing a hard filter (citizenship, clearance, required degree) | Skip |
| Not sure you meet it | Ask Claude or ChatGPT for a match score with the [Resume tailoring](../resume/tailoring.md) prompts, then decide |

Copy this template into your notes for each job:

```text
JD DECODE: [Company] [Role] [Req ID] [Link]
Posted: [date]  Ghost check passed: [Y/N]
Level signal: [new grad / I / II / unclear]
Minimums: [item] = Have / Partly / No
Preferred: [item] = Have / Partly / No
Experience line: [text]   Degree and grad window: [text]
Start date: [text]   Location and office policy: [text]
Visa words found: [sponsor / export control / US person / citizen / none]
Pay range: [text]
My 5 to 8 matching terms (only ones I have): [term], [term], [term]
Decision: Apply now / Apply with referral / Skip
```
