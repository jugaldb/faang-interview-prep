# Where to find jobs

For anyone searching for internships, new grad or 0 to 3 year roles. Set up the bookmarks and alerts below once, then run the 30-minute routine every weekday to catch roles posted in the last 24 hours.

## Where interviews actually come from

Find roles anywhere. Apply on the company's own site. Huntr's data on its users (job seekers across all fields) shows the gap.

| Route (Huntr Q2 2026) | Interview rate per application |
|---|---|
| Employer career sites | 6.42% |
| ATS domains (Workday, Greenhouse, Ashby and others) | 5.29% |
| Job boards and aggregators | 3.82% |

| Source (Huntr 2025 annual, 598,627 applications) | Response rate |
|---|---|
| Google Jobs | 11.3% |
| Wellfound | 6.0% |
| Glassdoor | 5.5% |
| Handshake | 5.1% |
| Welcome to the Jungle | 4.5% |
| Indeed | 4.5% |
| LinkedIn | 3.1% |
| ZipRecruiter | 2.8% |
| Dice | 0.35% |

Sources: [Huntr Q2 2026](https://huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q2-2026), [Huntr 2025 annual](https://huntr.co/research/2025-annual-job-search-trends-report). This is observational data, so read it as direction, not a guarantee.

Use sources in this order:

1. GitHub job lists and SWEList alerts (fastest signal for new grad and intern roles).
2. Company career pages with saved filters and alerts.
3. Google search across ATS domains, filtered to the last 24 hours.
4. LinkedIn saved searches for the last 24 hours (to discover roles, then apply on the company site).
5. Student and startup platforms: Handshake, Wellfound, YC Work at a Startup.
6. Aggregators as backup.

### Watch 10 to 20 companies with public job feeds

Many companies publish open roles as public JSON through their ATS. You can read it in a browser. No scraping and no login.

1. Open any job link from the company and find the board name in the URL. In `job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/...` the board is `anthropic`.
2. For Greenhouse, open `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/[board]/jobs`. Example: [Anthropic feed](https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/anthropic/jobs).
3. For Ashby, open `https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/[board]`. Example: [OpenAI feed](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/openai).
4. Ctrl+F for "new grad", "early career", "intern", "university".
5. Check your 10 to 20 target feeds once a week, or automate it with Jugal's n8n workflow ([n8n automation](../outreach/n8n-automation.md)).

## Google search across ATS sites

Jugal's method skips LinkedIn and searches the hiring systems directly. He went from 127 LinkedIn applications with zero responses to 65 applications, 22 responses, 8 interviews and 4 offers in four weeks ([Stop Applying to Ghost Jobs](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/stop-applying-to-ghost-jobs)).

The query below adds Ashby and Oracle Cloud, which his original list missed. Among open intern and new grad links on Oct 4, 2026: Workday 39.1%, Greenhouse 10.3%, Oracle Cloud 8.3%, Ashby 5.6%, iCIMS 4.5%, SmartRecruiters 2.3%, Lever 1.9%.

```text
("software engineer" OR "software developer") ("new grad" OR "early career" OR "university grad" OR "entry level" OR "engineer I") (site:myworkdayjobs.com OR site:job-boards.greenhouse.io OR site:boards.greenhouse.io OR site:jobs.ashbyhq.com OR site:jobs.lever.co OR site:oraclecloud.com OR site:icims.com OR site:smartrecruiters.com OR site:jobs.jobvite.com OR site:successfactors.com)
```

1. Paste the query into Google. Swap the title words for your role.
2. Add `&tbs=qdr:d` to the end of the results URL for the past 24 hours, or `&tbs=qdr:w` for the past week. Or use Tools, then "Past 24 hours".
3. Add a location in quotes if you need one, for example `"Seattle"`.
4. For one company, search its domain directly: `site:amazon.jobs "software development engineer"`.
5. Run it twice a day, morning and afternoon. Searching once a day is one of the mistakes Jugal lists in the same post.

> **Watch out:** the 24-hour filter goes by when Google found the page, which can differ from the posting date. Check the date on the job page itself. Workday job pages live on `myworkdayjobs.com`, so use that domain in `site:`.

Google's own job panel (search "[role] jobs" on google.com) had the highest response rate in Huntr's 2025 data (11.3%). Use it as a second discovery source.

## LinkedIn

Use LinkedIn to discover fresh roles. Apply on the company site when you can. LinkedIn as a source showed a 3.36% interview rate in Q2 2026 vs 6.42% for career sites ([Huntr](https://huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q2-2026)).

Build saved searches with these URL parameters (verified on the logged-out page, Oct 4, 2026):

| Parameter | What it does |
|---|---|
| `f_TPR=r86400` | Posted in the past 24 hours |
| `f_TPR=r604800` | Past week |
| `f_TPR=r2592000` | Past month |
| `sortBy=DD` | Newest first |
| `f_EA=true` | "Under 10 applicants" |
| `f_AL=true` | Easy Apply only (skip this one) |

```text
Past 24 hours, newest first:
https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=[role%20keywords]&location=[Location]&f_TPR=r86400&sortBy=DD

Past 24 hours, under 10 applicants:
https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=[role%20keywords]&location=[Location]&f_TPR=r86400&f_EA=true&sortBy=DD
```

1. Make one bookmark per role family, for example "software engineer new grad" and "machine learning engineer new grad".
2. Turn on the job alert for each saved search.
3. Look for the "Be an early applicant" label.
4. Use title keywords, not the "Entry level" filter. Posters set that tag, and it returned Senior roles in testing.
5. For your top roles posted in the last 3 days, apply and also message the team lead or hiring manager ([Jugal's routine](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-job-hunt-i-didnt-burn-out-doing)). Templates: [Outreach templates](../outreach/templates.md).

A ready example: [software engineer new grad, US, past 24 hours](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=software%20engineer%20new%20grad&location=United%20States&f_TPR=r86400&sortBy=DD).

## Freshness: why week one matters

Most big employers review on a rolling basis. A posted deadline is rarely the real one.

| Evidence (as of Oct 4, 2026) | Number |
|---|---|
| Summer 2027 listings already closed in early October | About 45% of all tracked, 47% of SWE |
| New grad listings closed over about 7 months | About 79% of 2,401 tracked |
| Jugal's Aug 20, 2026 internship links closed within about 6 weeks | At least 13 of 66, about 1 in 5 |
| Simplify README | "Many SWE internships only stay open for a few days" |
| Microsoft Leap postings (per Simplify) | Can close within 24 to 48 hours |

Jugal's arithmetic: if a company reviews 200 applications a week and fills interview slots in three weeks, your week-one application competes with 200 people. In week five it competes with everyone before you for what is left ([post](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/494-summer-2027-internships-are-already)). His example numbers are illustrative, not data.

Do this: apply within 3 days of posting, and write the posting age in your [tracker](application-strategy.md#tracker-template) so you can see your own median.

## Ghost jobs

A ghost job is a posting with no real intent to hire soon.

| Data point | Source |
|---|---|
| 18% to 22% of jobs listed on Greenhouse in a 2024 quarter were ghost jobs. Nearly 70% of companies on the platform posted at least one in Q2 2024 | [Fox6 on Greenhouse data](https://www.fox6now.com/news/ghost-jobs-greenhouse-analysis) |
| 39% of surveyed hiring managers said their company posted a fake listing in the past year. Top reasons: look open to talent, signal growth, reassure staff, collect resumes | [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/01/16/job-market-rife-fake-listing-ghosting-hiring-managers/) |
| 93% of job seekers applied to or suspect a ghost job | [Huntr Q1 2026](https://huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q1-2026) |
| "There are no official statistics on the magnitude of ghost jobs" | [Congressional Research Service](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IF12977.html) |

New rules (as of Oct 2026):

- **Ontario, Canada (from Jan 1, 2026):** employers with 25+ employees must say if a public posting is for an existing vacancy, include pay or a range no wider than $50,000, disclose AI use in hiring, and tell interviewed applicants within 45 days whether a decision was made ([Ontario ESA](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/recent-changes)).
- **New York:** a bill requiring employers with 100+ employees to say whether a role will be filled within 90 days passed both houses and awaited the governor's signature as of July 2026 ([SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/in/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/new-york-law-ghost-job-postings)). No signature was reported as of Oct 4, 2026.

### 60-second ghost-job check

- [ ] Posted within the last 7 days (LinkedIn time, Simplify Age column, or the ATS page date).
- [ ] The same req is live on the company's own careers site or ATS page. Only on an aggregator? Find it on the company site or skip.
- [ ] The same title has not been reposted over and over for months.
- [ ] The posting names a team, a location and concrete work, with a pay range where the law requires one.
- [ ] No "talent community", "future opportunities" or "evergreen" wording. Those mean pipeline building.

Fails two or more: skip it. Fails one: apply only if it is a top target, and pair it with outreach.

## Job scams

72% of surveyed job seekers ran into a job scam ([Huntr Q1 2026](https://huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q1-2026)). The FTC's rules of thumb: honest employers "will never ask you to pay to get a job", and "no honest potential employer will ever send you a check to deposit and then tell you to send on part of the money" ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams)). Run this check before you share any personal details:

- [ ] The posting exists on the company's own careers site or ATS.
- [ ] You had a real interview before any offer.
- [ ] Nobody asked you to pay for equipment, training, or "placement".
- [ ] The recruiter's email uses the company domain, and their LinkedIn profile matches.
- [ ] You did not share your SSN, passport, or bank details before a signed offer through the company's HR system.

## Your 30-minute daily search routine

Based on Jugal's weekday routine ([Stop Applying to Ghost Jobs](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/stop-applying-to-ghost-jobs)). Do it Monday to Friday.

- [ ] Open the SWEList email and the Simplify list for your track. Note roles 0 to 3 days old (3 min).
- [ ] Run the Google ATS query with the 24-hour filter (2 min).
- [ ] Open your LinkedIn past-24-hours saved search (2 min).
- [ ] Open 10 to 15 roles in tabs (3 min).
- [ ] Run the 60-second ghost check and the [JD decode](index.md#how-to-read-a-job-description) on each. Keep the best 3 to 5 (10 min).
- [ ] Apply to those 3 to 5 on the company site with a tailored resume (the rest of your time, see [Application strategy](application-strategy.md)).
- [ ] Log each one in your tracker the same minute.

Once a week (30 min):

- [ ] Search your top 20 companies' career pages and public job feeds directly.
- [ ] Check the Simplify Inactive list for companies that just closed, and note their posting month.
- [ ] Add new title variants you saw this week to your saved searches.
