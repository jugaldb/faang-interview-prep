# Offer negotiation resources

Articles, books, data sites and communities for offer negotiation, each with what to do with it. Free first. Paid ones are marked.

> **Watch out:** Several classic sources are old: Haseeb Qureshi (2016), Patrick McKenzie (2012), the Google ex-recruiter piece (2020), and Tech Interview Handbook's comp numbers (2021). The principles hold. Never quote their dollar figures as current.

## Read these first (about 90 minutes)

1. Read [Ten Rules for Negotiating a Job Offer](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/ten-rules-for-negotiating-a-job-offer-ee17cccbdab6/) by Haseeb Qureshi (2016). Write the ten rules on one index card and keep it next to you on every recruiter call.
2. Read [How Not to Bomb Your Offer Negotiation](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/how-not-to-bomb-your-offer-negotiation-c46bb9bc7dea/), his part 2, the night before your offer call. Note the closing move and when to use it.
3. Read the "never give a number first" part of [Salary Negotiation: Make More Money, Be More Valued](https://www.kalzumeus.com/2012/01/23/salary-negotiation/) by Patrick McKenzie (2012) before your first recruiter screen.
4. Read [Exactly what to say when recruiters ask you to name the first number](https://interviewing.io/blog/negotiate-salary-recruiter) by Aline Lerner. Practice the deflection out loud twice.
5. Read the [Compensation Breakdown](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/salary-negotiation/compensation-breakdown) and [How to Negotiate](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/salary-negotiation/how-to-negotiate) chapters on Hello Interview. Mark which components their table says are hard to move.
6. Open the Levels.fyi page for each company you are interviewing with (for example [Google](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer)). Copy the level, median, and vesting schedule into your tracker.

Then use this site's [process](index.md), [comp basics](comp-basics.md) and [scripts](scripts.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] Read the six "read these first" items.
- [ ] Bookmark the Levels.fyi page for every company in your pipeline.
- [ ] Find your school's offer deadline guidelines and save the link.
- [ ] Find 3 recent offers at the same level for each of your top 3 companies (Blind for the US, LeetCode Discuss or AmbitionBox for India, TechPays for Europe).
- [ ] Compare level ladders on the Levels.fyi level comparison before comparing pay.
- [ ] International students: run the Data Hub check and the FLAG wage lookup.
