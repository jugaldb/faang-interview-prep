# Resume rules and structure

For interns, new grads, and engineers with 0 to 3 years of experience. When you finish this page you will have a one-page base resume with the right sections in the right order.

## How a resume gets read

| Stage | Who reads it | What they check | What you control |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Application form | Knockout questions set by the recruiter ([Oracle Taleo docs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/taleo-enterprise/20b/otrec/candidate-prescreening.html)) | Work authorization, location, graduation date, degree | Truthful answers that match your resume |
| 2. Parser | The applicant tracking system (ATS) | Name, contact, dates, sections, plain text | One column, standard headings, a text PDF |
| 3. Search and ranking | Recruiter keyword search ([Greenhouse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/115004600186-Search-resumes-for-keywords)) and AI match grades ([Workday HiredScore](https://doc.workday.com/hiredscore/en-us/workday-hiredscore/recruiter-productivity-/reference--candidate-grades.html)) | The job's basic and preferred qualifications | The job's exact terms, backed by real work |
| 4. First human look | A recruiter: 5 to 10 seconds on the first pass ([Georgia Tech CS Resume Guide](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/sites/default/files/documents/2026/GT%20CS%20Resume%20Guide%20compressed_1.pdf)), median 31 seconds in a 2024 study ([interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/blog/are-recruiters-better-than-a-coin-flip-at-judging-resumes)) | Company names, school, outcomes in the top third | Your strongest evidence placed first |
| 5. Interviews | Engineers | Every line you wrote | Only claims you can defend for 5 minutes |

In the interviewing.io study, 76 recruiters judged about 1,000 resumes and were right only 55% of the time. Experience at a top-tier company was the strongest driver of a yes. You cannot fake a company name, so make your best evidence impossible to miss.

> **Watch out:** A resume is necessary, not sufficient. Jugal: "When I was searching for my first US role, I sent around 700 applications and got 3 interviews." He fixed it with people, not a better resume ([7 videos on networking your way to offers](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/7-videos-on-networking-your-way-to)). Pair this section with [referrals](../outreach/referrals.md).

## Ten rules

1. **Keep it to one page.** Laszlo Bock, then head of People Operations at Google, used "one page per 10 years of work experience" ([HC Magazine](https://www.hcamag.com/nz/news/general/the-worst-cv-mistakes-according-to-googles-hr-chief/141075)); Gayle Laakmann McDowell: "When you go onto two pages, you add weaker content to your resume, by definition" ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2014/10/02/how-can-i-get-my-resume-shortlisted-by-google-for-a-software-engineer-job/)). Google has no hard length rule but says "Keep it short" ([Google: how we hire](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)), and a UK CV is the exception ([country formats](templates.md#country-formats)).
2. **Use one column and standard headings.** Education, Experience, Projects, Skills. Parsers and recruiters both look for them ([how ATS works](ats.md)).
3. **Put your strongest evidence in the top third.** Most relevant role first, most relevant bullet first inside each role.
4. **Write every bullet as result, measure, method.** Google's wording: "accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]" ([Google: how we hire](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)). Full method: [writing bullets](writing-bullets.md).
5. **Tailor.** Keep one base resume, one version per role type, and a full pass for your top roles ([tailoring](tailoring.md)).
6. **Write only what you can defend.** Jugal: "Do not exaggerate. You'll be asked about everything in the interview" ([The New Grad and Internship prep for 2026](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-new-grad-and-internship-prep)).
7. **Send a text PDF named after you.** Use `Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf`. The Georgia Tech guide asks for your name in the file name.
8. **Ship zero typos.** Bock calls typos "deadly" because they read as carelessness. In one company's 2012 data, typos were the strongest signal of who got an offer ([interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/blog/resumes-suck-heres-the-data)). Small dataset, cheap fix.
9. **Make every link clickable and current.** Stale GitHub and LinkedIn profiles hurt you ([The Tech Resume Inside Out: common mistakes](https://thetechresume.com/samples/common-mistakes)).
10. **Match your LinkedIn.** Same titles, companies, and dates ([LinkedIn profile guide](../linkedin/index.md)).

## Section order by track

### Intern, no internship yet

| # | Section | What goes in | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header | Name, city and state, phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub | No photo, no date of birth |
| 2 | Education | School, degree, expected graduation month and year, GPA if 3.5/4.0 or higher, 4 to 6 relevant courses | High school only in your first semester or for a national-level award |
| 3 | Projects | 2 to 4 projects, each linked to GitHub or a demo | Your main evidence. Give it the most space |
| 4 | Experience | TA, grader, research, tutoring, freelance, part-time jobs | Non-CS jobs get 1 to 2 bullets |
| 5 | Skills | Languages, frameworks, tools, databases | Only what you can be interviewed on |
| 6 | Leadership and awards (optional) | Hackathon placements, club roles with numbers, scholarships | Show selectivity: "1 of 230 selected" |

Georgia Tech's rule: "If your Work Experience is unrelated to CS you should list your Programming Projects then your Work Experience."

### Intern with an internship, research, or TA role

| # | Section | What goes in | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header | As above | |
| 2 | Education | As above | Coursework only if there is room |
| 3 | Experience | Internship, research assistant, TA, co-op | 3 to 5 bullets for the most relevant role |
| 4 | Projects | 2 to 3 projects | Pick ones that show skills your experience does not |
| 5 | Skills | As above | |
| 6 | Leadership and awards (optional) | As above | |

### New grad

| # | Section | What goes in | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header | As above | |
| 2 | Education | Degree, school, graduation month and year, GPA if strong | Drop coursework if the page is full |
| 3 | Experience | Internships and co-ops first, then research and TA | 4 to 6 bullets for the most relevant role, 1 to 2 for others (Georgia Tech) |
| 4 | Projects | 2 to 3 projects | Tech Interview Handbook: "Include at least 2 projects" |
| 5 | Skills | Grouped lines | Order by the job description |
| 6 | Awards (optional) | Hackathons, scholarships, competitive programming ranks | One line each |

### Experienced, 1 to 3 years full-time

| # | Section | What goes in | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header | As above | |
| 2 | Summary (optional) | 1 to 2 lines, tailored to the role | Skip it if it says nothing a recruiter can check |
| 3 | Experience | Current role first, 3 to 6 bullets | Promotions as separate titles under one company |
| 4 | Skills | Grouped lines | |
| 5 | Projects (optional) | Only if they show something your job does not | |
| 6 | Education | Degree, school, year | Drop GPA and coursework |

The [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/) keeps Education above Experience until about 3 years. Decide by signal: if your employer name is stronger than your school, lead with Experience.

### Career changer into software

1. Put Projects right under the header (Georgia Tech rule above).
2. Keep past jobs, but rewrite bullets toward automation, data, tools you built, and scale you handled.
3. Put a bootcamp or non-CS degree after Projects.
4. Use a 1 to 2 line summary that names the target role and the bridge. This is the one case where a summary earns its space.

### Quick comparison

| Section | Intern | New grad | 1 to 3 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education | 2nd | 2nd | Last |
| Experience | 3rd or 4th | 3rd | 2nd or 3rd |
| Projects | 3rd | 4th | Optional |
| Skills | 5th | 5th | 4th |
| Summary | No | No | Optional |
| GPA | If 3.5+ | If 3.5+ | Drop |
| Coursework | Yes, if room | Rarely | No |

## The header

```text
[FULL NAME]
[City, ST] | [phone] | [personal.email@domain.com] | linkedin.com/in/[handle] | github.com/[handle]
```

1. List four or five contact items. The Tech Resume Inside Out suggests about four: phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub.
2. Write city and state only. Tech Interview Handbook: "Just enough for recruiters to determine if you are a local or international candidate."
3. Use a personal email. Never a work email or work phone (Tech Interview Handbook).
4. Show readable link text such as `linkedin.com/in/handle` and make it clickable.
5. Add a portfolio site only if it shows work that GitHub does not.
6. Leave out photo, date of birth, age, marital status, nationality, and street address for US and UK roles.
7. Leave visa status off by default. The application form asks. See [international students](../jobs/international-students.md) for the exceptions.

## Education

| Item | Include when | Write it like |
|---|---|---|
| School, degree, major | Always | `B.S. in Computer Science, [University], [City, ST]` |
| Graduation date | Always as a student | `Expected May 2027`. NVIDIA and Google EMEA intern postings ask for graduation month and year on the resume |
| GPA | 3.5/4.0 or above (Georgia Tech, Tech Interview Handbook), or above 4.3 on a 5-point scale | `GPA: 3.72/4.00` |
| GPA on a 10-point scale | Only if strong. Show the original scale, do not convert | `CGPA: 8.7/10` |
| Relevant coursework | Room left and few projects or internships. Google says recent grads can include coursework ([how we hire](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/)) | 4 to 6 upper-level courses on one line |
| High school | First semester only, or a national-level result (olympiad medal, top test rank) | One line |
| Exchange or study abroad | Optional | One line under the main degree |

> **Tip:** Google intern postings ask for a transcript too ([internships](../internships/index.md)). Keep an English transcript PDF ready next to your resume.

## Experience: what counts

| Type | Counts as Experience? | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Internship or co-op | Yes | Experience |
| Teaching assistant, grader, teaching fellow | Yes. Harvard's 2026 CS example lists a Teaching Fellow role under experience ([PDF](https://cdn-careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/161/2026/07/Harvard-College-CS-Resume-Example.pdf)) | Experience |
| Research assistant | Yes | Experience |
| Paid freelance or contract work | Yes | Experience |
| Open-source programs (Google Summer of Code, MLH Fellowship, LFX Mentorship) | Yes | Experience |
| Hackathon | Usually a project | Projects, placement in Awards |
| Non-tech part-time job | Yes, 1 to 2 bullets | Below Projects if your CS work is thin |
| Club leadership | Only with scale (members, events, money) | Leadership |

Bullet count per role: 4 to 6 for high-relevance positions and 1 to 2 for low-relevance ones (Georgia Tech). On a full student page, 3 to 4 for your best role is a practical floor.

## Skills

```text
Languages: [Python, Java, C++, SQL, TypeScript]
Frameworks: [React, Node.js, Spring Boot, FastAPI, PyTorch]
Cloud and tools: [Amazon Web Services (AWS), Docker, Git, GitHub Actions, Linux]
Databases: [PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB]
```

1. List only what you could answer interview questions about. Jugal: "If the role asks for Kafka and you have never used Kafka, do not add Kafka" ([I Asked Claude to Make My Resume Unrejectable](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/i-asked-claude-to-make-my-resume)).
2. Leave out soft skills. Georgia Tech: "Do not list soft skills in this section."
3. Leave out skill bars and ratings. They "can only backfire" ([The Tech Resume Inside Out](https://thetechresume.com/samples/common-mistakes)).
4. Order each line by the job description's priority.
5. Spell out an acronym once: "Amazon Web Services (AWS)" (Tech Interview Handbook).
6. Add AI coding tools only if you used them on real work. Amazon's Summer 2027 SDE intern posting lists "Experience using AI-assisted development tools in academic or professional settings" as a basic qualification ([posting](https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10552937/software-development-engineer-intern-summer-2027-usa)).

## Certifications and courses

Certificates add searchable terms and recognizable provider names at entry level. Jugal's rules from [The "Borrowed Logo" Strategy](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/the-borrowed-logo-strategy-how-to):

1. Put them in their own section, clearly labelled. Never under Experience.
2. Pick 3 to 7 that match the job. Do not dump every course.
3. Use one line each:

```text
CERTIFICATIONS
[Certificate name], [Provider], [Year]
[Certificate name], [Provider], [Year]
```

Certificates rarely beat projects. Jugal's heading from [Top 30 AI Certifications](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/top-30-ai-certifications-you-need): "Certificates Get the Interview and Projects Get the Offer."

| Source | Cost | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| [CS50x](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) | Free | Finish the problem sets and final project, then list the certificate if you have no CS degree |
| [AWS Skill Builder](https://skillbuilder.aws/) | Free digital training, paid exams | Do the cloud fundamentals path before applying to cloud-heavy roles |
| [Microsoft Learn credentials](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/) | Free learning, paid exams | Fundamentals exams are a cheap first credential |
| [Google Skills](https://www.skills.google/) | Free and paid labs | Use the cloud and AI skill badges for cloud or AI roles |
| [freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/) | Free | Project-based certifications for web roles |
| [HackerRank skills certification](https://www.hackerrank.com/skills-verification) | Free | Timed tests in problem solving, Python, SQL. Doubles as [online assessment](../online-assessments/index.md) practice |

## Summary or objective

| Situation | Summary? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US student or new grad | No | Gayle: "No summaries." Georgia Tech: "When in doubt, leave it out" |
| 1 to 3 years | Optional, 1 to 2 lines, tailored | Tech Interview Handbook allows a short summary for experienced candidates |
| Career changer | Yes, 1 to 2 lines | Explains the jump |
| UK CV | Yes, a short profile | [Prospects](https://www.prospects.ac.uk/careers-advice/cvs-and-cover-letters/how-to-write-a-cv) |

```text
Weak:   Motivated computer science student seeking challenging opportunities to grow.
Strong: Backend engineer, 2 years on payments APIs in Java and AWS; cut p99 latency [60%] on a [5k] requests/sec service.
```

## What to cut

| Cut | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Photo, date of birth, age, marital status, father's name, "Declaration" | Not expected in US or UK; invites bias | [The Tech Resume Inside Out](https://thetechresume.com/samples/common-mistakes), [Prospects](https://www.prospects.ac.uk/careers-advice/cvs-and-cover-letters/how-to-write-a-cv), [Jugal](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/4-resume-templates-based-on-your) |
| Street address | City and state are enough | Tech Interview Handbook |
| "References available on request" | Wasted line | The Tech Resume Inside Out |
| Objective line | Says nothing checkable | Gayle, Georgia Tech |
| Soft skills in Skills | Unverifiable | Georgia Tech |
| Skill bars and ratings | "Can only backfire" | The Tech Resume Inside Out |
| "Responsible for..." | Duties, not results | [Amazon recruiters](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/amazon-job-application-resume-writing-tips), [Jugal](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/how-i-turned-my-resume-into-a-job) |
| Internal project code names | The reader cannot decode them | The Tech Resume Inside Out |
| High school after your first semester | Weaker than college content | Georgia Tech |
| Every tool you touched once | You will be asked about it | Jugal |
| Hidden white text or AI prompts | Detected and penalized | [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/hidden-ai-prompts-in-resume) |
| Columns, icons, images, tables | Break parsing | [Workday docs](https://doc.workday.com/admin-guide/en-us/human-capital-management/recruiting/candidates/set-up-prospects-and-candidates/hdc1552497830785.html), [OpenResume](https://www.open-resume.com/resume-parser) |

## Build your base resume this weekend

1. Watch Jugal's walkthrough of the resume he used for Amazon: [This Resume Got Me Into Amazon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9Gcv9PByAI) (about 10 minutes).
2. Dump everything into a plain doc: every project, internship, TA role, hackathon, club role, award, and pull request. Jugal: "Write down every single thing you've ever done professionally. Everything. We'll cut it down later" ([Your 6-Week Amazon Interview Roadmap](https://jugaldb.substack.com/p/amazon-is-still-hiring-after-the)).
3. Open a template. Default: [Jake's Resume on Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/jakes-resume/syzfjbzwjncs). Other options and setup steps: [templates](templates.md).
4. Fill the header with the rules above.
5. Pick your section order from the track tables above.
6. Write each bullet with the X, Y, Z method ([writing bullets](writing-bullets.md#write-one-bullet-in-5-minutes)).
7. Choose 2 to 4 projects ([how to choose projects](writing-bullets.md#choose-your-projects)).
8. Write three to four Skills lines.
9. Cut to one page. Remove the weakest bullet first, not the font size.
10. Export a PDF, name it `Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf`, and run the [10-minute parse test](ats.md#test-your-pdf-in-10-minutes).
11. Run the [final checklist](checklist.md).
12. Get two human reviews ([free review sources](checklist.md#free-review-sources)).
13. Save this as your base. Make role-type copies next ([tailoring](tailoring.md)).

> **Tip:** Keep a running "wins" doc. Every time you ship something, write one line with the number. Your next resume update then takes 20 minutes instead of a weekend.

## Free reading, in order

1. [Tech Interview Handbook: Resume](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/): free SWE resume guide by Yangshun Tay, updated Aug 2026. How to use it: read the ATS and keyword sections, then run its plain-text test on your PDF.
2. [Georgia Tech CS Resume Guide (2026 PDF)](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/sites/default/files/documents/2026/GT%20CS%20Resume%20Guide%20compressed_1.pdf): a university CS guide with section rules and a CS verb list. How to use it: copy its bullet-count rule and verb list.
3. [Harvard College CS Resume Example (2026 PDF)](https://cdn-careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/161/2026/07/Harvard-College-CS-Resume-Example.pdf): an annotated student CS resume. How to use it: compare your section order and how it lists a TA role.
4. [The Tech Resume Inside Out: free sample chapters](https://thetechresume.com/samples/common-mistakes) by Gergely Orosz. How to use it: run your resume against each mistake. The full book is paid; developers without a job can [request a free copy](https://thetechresume.com/complimentary-copy).
5. [Gayle Laakmann McDowell on Fortune (2014)](https://fortune.com/2014/10/02/how-can-i-get-my-resume-shortlisted-by-google-for-a-software-engineer-job/): short rules from the author of Cracking the Coding Interview. How to use it: apply the bullet-length and quantify rules. Skip the dated language advice.
6. [interviewing.io: Are recruiters better than a coin flip?](https://interviewing.io/blog/are-recruiters-better-than-a-coin-flip-at-judging-resumes): the 2024 study above. How to use it: read it once to see why the top third matters.
